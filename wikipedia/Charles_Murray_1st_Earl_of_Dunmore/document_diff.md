+== Court and political career ==
+
+Charles Murray was a politician who obtained a court position under Princess Anne in 1683.
+In 1685, he became the Master of the Horse to Duchess Mary of Modena and retained his position when the Duke of York ascended the throne as King James II and VII in the same year.
+He ran for office and was elected, and he voted in support of James II in Parliament.
