# Marek Prawda

Marek Władysław Prawda (born 1 October 1956 in Kielce) is a Polish sociologist and diplomat; since January 2024 he serves as Undersecretary of State of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Between April 2016 and 2021 he was Head of Representation – Poland at European Commission.

Prawda studied economics at the University of Leipzig and sociology at the Institute of Philosophy and Sociology of the Polish Academy of Science.
In 1979 he obtained a degree in economic studies and in 1984 a PhD degree in sociology.
Until 1990 he has been working at the Polish Academy of Science.
He was member of Solidarity.

In 1992 he started his diplomatic career as First Secretary to the Embassy of Poland in Bonn, Germany.
In 2000 and 2005 he had been Director at the Secretariat of the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

From 2001 to 2005, he served as Ambassador to Sweden, from 2006 to 2012 as Ambassador to Germany and from 2012 to 2016 as Ambassador to the European Union.

In February 2016, Prawda ended his term.
In April 2016, he was appointed by Jean-Claude Juncker as Head of the European Representation in Warsaw.
He ended his term in 2021.

In January 2024, he was appointed Undersecretary of State at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

While Ambassador to Germany, Prawda has been an important promoters of the German-Polish dialogue.
In 2012 he has been awarded the Grand Cross of the Order of Merit of the Federal Republic of Germany and the Order of Merit of Brandenburg.

Prawda has published a number of articles on Polish-German relations in newspapers and magazines.

Besides Polish, he speaks English, German, French, Swedish and Russian.

== Education and academic background ==

Marek Prawda began his studies in 1975 at an institute located in Warsaw and studied economics between 1975 and 1979.
He was a doctoral student starting in 1979 and obtained a doctorate in humanities in the field of work sociology.
His doctoral dissertation was titled 'The Life Cycle of the Individual and the Value of Work' and was published by Ossolineum.
He was a student of Jerzy Holzer.
Marek Prawda stayed at the University of Hamburg for study purposes from 1987 to 1989.
He received a research scholarship awarded between 1987 and 1989.

== Early career and Solidarity activism ==

Marek Prawda dedicated himself to research from 1979 to 1990 and joined Solidarity in 1980.
He was a scientific employee at the Institute of Philosophy and Sociology of the Polish Academy of Sciences from 1984 to 1990.
His work in NSZZ Solidarność took place during the 1980s.
Marek Prawda worked at the Institute for Political Studies, which is part of the Academy of Sciences in Warsaw, from 1990 to 1992.
He was a scientific employee at the Department of German Studies in the Institute of Political Studies of the Polish Academy of Sciences from 1990 to 1992.
He was a member of the local Citizens' Committee in 1989.
The committee was located in Żoliborz, Warsaw.
He acted on behalf of the Citizens' Committee (KO).
He coordinated aid for refugees from East Germany arriving in Poland in the autumn of 1989.

== Diplomatic and ministerial service ==

In 2001, Marek Prawda temporarily became deputy director of the Western Europe department in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Poland and remained in his position until 2005.
He was recalled as Poland's representative to the EU in February 2016, a premature recall since his regular term would have ended in autumn 2016.

== Teaching and personal life ==

Prawda was a lecturer who taught at Collegium Civitas in Warsaw, the Vistula School of Finance and Business, and the European University Viadrina in Frankfurt an der Oder.
He is married and has two daughters.

== Diplomatic career ==

He served as the general secretary between 1991 and 1992.
He worked at the Embassy of Poland in Bonn for six years, serving as Counsellor and as Chargé d'affaires from September to December 1995.
He was a deputy director of the Western Europe Department from 1998 to 1999 and served as director of the Western Europe Department from 1999 to 2001.
He served as director of the Minister's Secretariat in 2001.
He returned to the position of secretary-general's director in August 2006 and held the position until August 31, 2012.
This appointment occurred in September 2012, and he ceased his official duties on February 29, 2016.
The individual was appointed on January 9, 2024, with responsibilities including Eastern and European policy and EU enlargement and neighborhood policy.
He represented the minister at meetings of the Committee on European Affairs.

He was responsible for recruiting Polish citizens for the European External Action Service and for international institutions and organizations.

Marek Prawda wrote for the magazine 'Borussia', which is published in Olsztyn and aims to discover and preserve shared German-Polish cultural heritage.
He belonged to the international prize committee of the Adalbert Foundation.
Prawda was awarded the DIALOG Prize at the 25th anniversary conference of the German-Polish Society in Danzig in 2016, where Gesine Schwan delivered the speech of commendation.

== Awards and honors ==

He became a member of the board of the Euro-Atlantic Association.
The recipient received the Federal Association of German-Polish Societies Dialogue Award in 2016.
The recipient received the European Institute of Culture and Media 'Polonicus' Award in 2019.
