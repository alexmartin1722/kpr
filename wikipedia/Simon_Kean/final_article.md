# Simon Kean

Simon Kean (born 11 January 1989) is a former Canadian professional boxer. He announced his retirement on 30 January 2024. As an amateur he qualified for the 2012 Summer Olympics along with countryman Custio Clayton.

== Physical attributes ==

The athlete weighs over 91 kg and is approximately 1.96 meters tall.

== Professional debut ==

Kean made his professional debut on November 6, 2015, against Hungary's Bodo Balazs.
The match ended in a technical knockout in the first round.

== Early title wins ==

He won the IBO Intercontinental Championship in June 2017.
He became the WBC Francophone Champion in June 2018.

== Carman rivalry ==

Kean lost to Dillon Carman by knockout in October 2018.
He won the rematch by TKO in June 2019.

== WBC International Silver title ==

On December 7, 2019, Simon Kean won the vacant WBC International Silver heavyweight title by defeating Sergey Lyakhovich via technical knockout in the 10th round of a 10-round match held in Canada.
At the time of the fight, Lyakhovich was a 43-year-old former world champion from Belarus with a professional record of 27-7.

== Fight against Joseph Parker ==

Kean lost to Joseph Parker in a fight that took place on October 28, 2023, in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, as an undercard to the Tyson Fury vs.
Francis Ngannu bout.
The loss occurred via a stoppage in the third round when the referee stopped the fight after the count following a precise punch from Parker that knocked out Kean.
