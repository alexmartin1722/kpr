# Aletheia University

Aletheia University (after Greek ἀλήθεια, 'truth') () is a private university in Tamsui, New Taipei City and Madou, Tainan in Taiwan. It was founded by George Leslie Mackay as Oxford College. It has close links to the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan, and is one of the oldest institutions of higher education in Taiwan.

In 1872, Dr. George Leslie Mackay, a missionary from the Presbyterian Church in Canada, designed and supervised the construction of the original school building.
He named it "Oxford College" as a gesture of thanks to the residents of Oxford County in Ontario, Canada, who had made financial donations.
It was completed in 1882.
The curriculum included courses in theology, Bible studies, sociology, logic, classical Chinese literature, Chinese history, natural sciences, and basic medicine, anatomy, and clinical practices.
Oxford College was an early example of western-style institution and general education in Taiwan.

The Northern Synod of the Presbyterian Church in Taiwan was the founder of the later university, which the group planned in 1959 to establish at the location of Oxford College.
In August 1965, the Ministry of Education gave approval for the Tamsui Institute of Industrial & Business Administration.
In April 1971, its name was changed to Tamsui Oxford College.

In 1991, 18.8 hectares of land was purchased to create a branch school in Matou, Tainan county.
The school received permission to become a four-year college called the Tamsui Oxford University College in August 1994.
The Matou branch began accepting students, with an emphasis on two-year technical education, in February 1997.

In August 1999, the school was renamed Aletheia University, after the Greek word for truth.
As of 2023, the university has 22 departments and offers nine vocational Bachelor's programs and eight Master's programs.
They are organized into five colleges including Humanities, Finance and Economics, Management, "Tourism, Leisure, and Sports", and Information Sciences and Business Intelligence.

== Founding and Early Missionary Work ==

The school's history dates back to March 9, 1872, when Canadian Presbyterian missionary James Laidlaw Maxwell landed in Tamsui, a base established by Westerners in northern Taiwan.
On that date, Dr.
George Leslie Mackay also landed in Tamsui and used Paotai Pu as his mission base.
In 1872, George Leslie Mackay raised funds from a Protestant church in Canada for the construction of the main school building.
The schoolhouse was built for purposes of missionary work, education, and medical care.
Dr.
George Leslie Mackay engaged in medical work and agricultural work.
Teaching activities took place in his rented residence and outdoors for eight years.

== Establishment of Oxford College ==

A school called Lixuetang Academy was established on July 26, 1882, and later generations referred to it as Oxford School.

== Mid-20th Century Planning and Approval ==

Higher education planning was initiated on February 14, 1959, with the goal to establish a comprehensive university in the future [cite: 20].
Mr.

Plans were made to open five departments: Mathematics, Biology, Nursing, Home Economics, and Chemistry [cite: 20].

== Expansion and Reorganization in the 1990s ==

The institution was reorganized in 1994.
A foreign language department, a mathematics department, and a sports management department were established in 1995.
The second campus was completed in 1996 and offers a two-year technical program in Information Management Technology and a two-year technical program in Applied Foreign Languages.

== Program Consolidation and Tainan Campus Closure (2015–2016) ==

In 2015, the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science and the Department of Applied Mathematics at the Taipei campus were merged into the Department of Finance and Actuarial Science, which established a Financial Information Group and an Actuarial and Insurance Management Group.

In 2016, the Tainan campus stopped recruiting students, the Department of Sports Health and Leisure was discontinued, the Bachelor's Degree Program in Tourism Culture and Health Industry was discontinued, and the Bachelor's Degree Program in Tourism and Service Industry was discontinued.

== Recent Developments and Merger Proposals ==

Aletheia University is a university of the Taiwan Presbyterian Church whose predecessor was the Tamsui College of Business Administration.
The institution was officially reorganized into Truth University in 1999, a change approved by the Ministry of Education that made Aletheia University a comprehensive university.
The Department of Aviation Service Management at the Taipei campus was renamed in 2018.
A merger proposal involving Mackay Medical College, Mackay Junior College, and Trinity University was approved on March 6, 2023, at the 76th Northern Taiwan Presbyterian Church Assembly.
A task force for legal entity merger was established to execute future processes and hold meetings.
Truth University operates independently in terms of academic affairs and has not merged with the other two schools yet.
There is potential for various inter-school administrative integration in the future.
Reports of an imminent merger are speculation by some media outlets.

== Early foundations and historic buildings ==

The Ma Jie Former Residence, also known as the White Palace, is a Spanish-style building constructed in 1875 by Dr.
Ma Jie.
The Li Xuetang Academy, the earliest Li Xuetang in Taiwan, was built in 1882 and currently houses the Truth University History Museum (Main Building).
A film depicting the process of indigenous people's resistance turning into acceptance is shown regularly in the Li Xuetang College.
The Missionary Hall is a Spanish-style building constructed in 1875 that was originally used as a dormitory for missionaries.
In modern times, the building served as the former principal's residence.
The building is now planned to be the Missionary Hall of Truth University.
The hall serves as a memorial for missionaries who evangelized in Taiwan and provides dining services.
The Red Building was constructed in 1906.
Both buildings were constructed by Reverend Wu William.

== Historical uses of the Ma Jie Former Residence ==

During World War II, a church established the Ma Kuai Residence as a shelter for abandoned women.
The building later became the boys' dormitory of Tanjiang High School.
After the war, Dr.
Hsieh Yui-lin returned to Tamsui and resided there until 1952.
When the Ma Kai family returned to Taiwan, they stayed at the Dr.
Ma Kai residence.
After the founding of Truth University, the Dr.
Ma Kai residence became a library, an internship hotel, and a guest house for scholars.

== Controversies ==

Rumors circulated in late July 2017 stating that Truth University would merge with Mackay Medical College and Mackay Nursing College, that the merger would be completed before 2020, and that the merged institution would be renamed Mackay University.
Mackay Medical College confirmed that these rumors were false.
The results of the Academic Aptitude Test were released in January 2021, and media reports covered an incident at the Taiwan Literature Center of the Southern Research Center of Truth University before the test recommendations were announced for candidates.
Some media outlets published sensational articles in late May to late July 2022 just before the 2022 university registration period.
These articles claimed that all private schools under the National Zhenyuan system would be closed and that the German Zhenyuan private school specifically would be closed.
Truth University clarified that these claims were false information, stated it has not been closed or ceased to exist, stated its finances are not problematic, and stated its academic affairs are operating normally.
