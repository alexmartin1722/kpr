+== Combat record and honors ==
+
+The division liberated fifteen regions of the Soviet Union, fought over 11,000 kilometers, and liberated over 2,000 populated places.
+The unit captured approximately 20,000 enemy soldiers and officers, 127 tanks, 30 aircraft, over 1,500 guns and mortars, and more than 2,000 machine guns.
+Approximately 15,000 soldiers, sergeants, and officers of the division were awarded orders and medals of the Soviet Union.
+The last battle occurred during the destruction of remaining enemy forces and took place in the city of Mladá Boleslav.
