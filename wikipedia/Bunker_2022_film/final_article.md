# Bunker 2022 film

Bunker is a 2022 horror thriller film directed by Adrian Langley and written by Michael Huntsman. The film stars Roger Clark, Luke Baines, Patrick Moltane, Kayla Radomski and Julian Feder.

== Details ==

Bunker is an American horror film.
The original language is English.
The duration is 108 minutes.

A small group of British soldiers entered a German underground bunker through a barricaded door.
An exhausted deserter soldier was inside the bunker.
In the bunker, paranoid soldiers face a specter.
The phenomena encountered were inexplicable.
The military personnel gradually began to lose their sanity.

Adrian Langley directed, Michael Huntsman is the screenwriter, and both Jame Huntsman and Patrick Rizzotti produced the film.
Buffalo FilmWorks produced the film.
Matthew Quinn is the cinematographer.
Andrew Morgan Smith is the composer.

The film premiered October 8, 2022 at Buffalo International Film Festival, where it won an award in 2022.

Film aggregator Rotten Tomatoes holds an approval rating of 29% based on seven reviews. Bobby LePire at Film Threat awarded it 8 out of 10, Jonathan Hickman at Newnan Times-Herald gave it a 7 out of 10 while Roger Moore at Movie Nation scored in 1 out of 4.
