# 2019 World Mixed Curling Championship

The 2019 World Mixed Curling Championship was held from 12 to 19 October in Aberdeen, Scotland.

In the final, Canada's Colin Kurz defeated Germany's Andy Kapp 6-5. It was Canada's second consecutive title at the World Mixed, having won in 2018. It was also Germany's first spot on the podium, having never made it past the quarterfinals in past years.

== Overview ==

The 5th World Mixed Curling Championship was held in Aberdeen, United Kingdom, and was organized by the World Curling Federation (WCF) and the Scottish Curling Federation.
The tournament was held for the fifth time.

== Venue ==

The venue was Curl Aberdeen, which is located in the western part of the city.
The competitions were held at the Curl Aberdeen Centre.

== Format ==

The matches were played differently from previous championships.

The distance for the test throws was measured in centimeters.
