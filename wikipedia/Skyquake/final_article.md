# Skyquake

A skyquake is a phenomenon where a loud banging sound is reported to originate from the sky. The sound may cause noticeable vibration in the ceiling or across a particular room. Those who experience skyquakes typically do not have a clear explanation for what caused them and they are perceived as mysterious.

They have been heard in several locations around the world, including the banks of the river Ganges, Marwadi/Marawadi (मरवड़ी/मराड़ी) village in Himachal Pradesh, Hanswar, Uttar Pradesh, the East Coast and inland Finger Lakes of the United States, the city of Hudson, Wisconsin, the Magic Valley in south-central Idaho, the Municipality of Anchorage and Southcentral Alaska, Colombia, Southern Canada, as well as areas of the North Sea, Japan, Finland, Australia, Italy, Ireland, India, The Netherlands, Norway, Tierra del Fuego in Argentina, the United Kingdom, Mexico and Indonesia (particularly Jakarta and Java).

== Sea roar ==

Sea roar refers to a booming sound heard from the sea that is typically heard near the coast, where most ocean sounds are heard.
Ocean sounds typically contain low-frequency sounds, and the sound of the roar of the sea is described as 'guro-guro', 'go', or 'gogo-gogo-go...'.
The sound of the roar of the sea is compared to thunder and is often heard on open coastlines, though it can also be heard inland, such as on mountain tops, and is audible in open areas regardless of whether they are coastal or inland.
The sound of the ocean roar is heard differently depending on wind direction; when the wind blows from the sea, the sound is heard loudly, but when the wind blows in the opposite direction from land to sea, the sound cannot be heard.
When a sea roar occurs, the weather is rougher in the open sea compared to the coast and appears gloomy.
The cause of the roar of the sea is the sound produced by waves breaking that are generated by rough weather conditions in the open ocean.
The sound of the roar of the sea can travel up to 10 kilometers and can travel more than 10 kilometers.

The sound is louder when the clouds are thicker, and there is a tendency for the sound to be louder with thicker clouds.
Ocean sounds from the open sea cannot be heard when the coastline is rough, as the sound of ocean waves on the open sea is drowned out by the noise of coastal waves when the coastline is rough.
Ocean sounds can only be heard when coastal waves are relatively calm, and ocean sounds cannot be heard if the coastline is rough.
