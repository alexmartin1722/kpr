# Ketil Haugsand

Ketil Are Haugsand (born 13 June 1947, Oslo, Norway) is a Norwegian harpsichordist and conductor.

Haugsand studied at the conservatories in Trondheim and Oslo, and later studied in Prague and Haarlem.
In 1973, he earned his solo diploma.
In 1975, he was awarded the Prix d'Excellence at the Amsterdam conservatory, where he studied under Gustav Leonhardt, and he completed his studies at the Amsterdam Conservatory.

Haugsand is now a world-renowned harpsichordist and has toured extensively in Europe, Israel and the United States.
Major recordings include Johann Sebastian Bach's Goldberg Variations and several recordings with the Norwegian Baroque Orchestra.
He was professor of music at the Norwegian Academy of Music from 1974 until 1995.
Since 1995, he has been a professor at the Hochschule für Musik (Academy of Music) in Cologne, Germany.

== Awards and competitions ==

He won the fifth prize in 1980 at an international harpsichord competition that was part of the Festival Musica Antiqua in Bruges.

== Performance career ==

Haugsand worked on his career since 1969.
He performed at the Komische Oper in Berlin and at numerous concerts at major festivals in Europe, the USA, and Israel.
Haugsand performed with Jaap Schröder, Wieland Kuijken, Barthold Kuijken, Marie Leonhardt, and Frans Brüggen.
He performed frequently with Laurence Dreyfus.

== Conducting and artistic direction ==

Haugsand conducted the Norwegian Baroque Orchestra, the ArteReal Ensemble, the Stavanger Symphony Orchestra, and the NRK Radio Orchestra in Oslo.
He is the artistic director of Ensemble Arte Real and the Norwegian Baroque Orchestra.

== Teaching ==

Ketil Are Haugsand is a professor at the Cologne University of Music and Dance and taught at the Norwegian Academy of Music in Oslo.
He teaches at summer schools, including the Academy of Ancient Music in Tomar, Portugal, and the Ringve International Summer Course in Trondheim, Norway.
His teaching subjects include harpsichord, continuo, early organ repertoire, piano, and training for baroque ensembles.
He gave annual courses in early music in Lisbon and Porto as a founding member of the Academia de Musica Antiga de Lisboa.

== Jury service ==

Haugsand frequently serves as a jury member for international competitions, including events held in Leipzig, Warsaw, and Prague.
He was a member of the jury for an international harpsichord competition held in Bruges in 2004 and 2007.

== Instrument building ==

Ketil Haugsand is a harpsichord builder who tunes a harpsichord.
He is often consulted for designing new instruments and for restoring old instruments.
