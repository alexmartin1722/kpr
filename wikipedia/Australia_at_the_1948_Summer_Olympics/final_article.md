# Australia at the 1948 Summer Olympics

Australia competed at the 1948 Summer Olympics in London, England. 75 competitors, 66 men and 9 women, took part in 52 events in 11 sports. Australian athletes have competed in every Summer Olympic Games.

== Overview ==

Australia participated in the 14th Olympic Games, which were held in London from July 20 to August 14, 1948.
The 1948 London Olympics took place from July 29 to August 14, 1948.
This was Australia's ninth appearance at the Summer Olympics.
The number of medals won increased significantly compared to previous games.

== Flag bearer ==

Les McKay was the flag bearer at the opening ceremony and carried the flag at the opening ceremony.

The Australian team won 13 medals at the 1948 Summer Olympics.
Mervyn Wood won the gold medal.
Australia won 2 gold medals, 6 silver medals, and 5 bronze medals.
The number of medals won increased compared to previous games, when only one bronze medal was won.
