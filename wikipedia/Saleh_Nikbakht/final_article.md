# Saleh Nikbakht

Saleh Nikbakht () is an Iranian lawyer and academic. He is the spokesman for the Society of Political Prisoners in Iran.

Nikbakht is from Saqqez and studied law and hold a doctoral degree from Tehran University.
Mohammad Saleh Nikbakht was born in Kurdistan.
He is an active member of the Kurdish community residing in Tehran.
Nikbakht was involved in controversial cases including Hashem Aghajari, Abbas Abdi, Emad Baghi and 2009 presidential election detainees.
Nikbakht is representing former deputy foreign minister Mohsen Aminzadeh, ex-government spokesman Abdollah Ramezanzadeh, former deputy economy minister Mohsen Safai-Farahani and former vice-president Mohammad Ali Abtahi.

Nikbakht is the lawyer for the family of Mahsa Amini, whose death in 2022 triggered widespread protests throughout Iran.
In August 2023, he was subsequently charged with "propaganda against the state" by the Islamic Revolutionary Court in Tehran; his lawyer has alleged these charges stemmed from his interviews with domestic and foreign media outlets.

== Legal career and activism ==

Mohammad Saleh Nikbakht is a prominent Iranian figure and an activist for human rights.
He represented cases related to the 1388 protests, including those of Behzad Nabavi, Maziar Bahari, and Jafar Panahi.
Nikbakht acted as a lawyer for Bank Saderat in the Fazl Khodadad case, which occurred in the early 1970s of the Solar Hijri calendar.
He is one of two lawyers for Jafar Panahi and a founding member of the United Kurdish Front.

In October 2023, Saleh Nikbakht was called to court on the charge of propagandizing against the Islamic Republic of Iran during an interview with Persian-language TV channels outside Iran about the case of Mahsa Amini, and was finally sentenced to one year in prison and two years of being banned from using cyberspace.
The legal case involves charges against national security.
In this regard, his lawyer stated that there is no evidence of propaganda against the regime in Nikbakht's interviews.
The lawyer spoke to BBC Persian on 15 Mordad 1401 (August 6, 2022).

== Publications and commentary ==

The article titled "Discount in a National Case" was authored by Mohammad-Saleh Nikbakht and published in Iran Newspaper on 7 Azar 1396 (Shamsi calendar).
The article discusses a discount granted in a national case and addresses gangsterism from the perspective of a legal expert.
The news was republished by IRNA news agency, Isna news agency, and Tabnak news agency.
The second republishing date was 29 Mehr 1399 (Shamsi calendar).
