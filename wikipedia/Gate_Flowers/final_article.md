# Gate Flowers

Gate Flowers () is a South Korean hard rock band. The band currently consists of Park Geunhong, Yeom Seungshik, Lee Insan and Jeon Sangjin. Since their formation in 2005, the band has released the studio album Times (2012).

== Etymology ==

The subject is referred to as 'Wolae'.

Gate Flowers was formed in 2005 in Seoul, but disbanded in 2006, and reunited in 2008.
In October 2009, they were honoured rookies on EBS Space.
The group was selected as Hello Rookie of the Month on EBS Space 공감 Hello Rookie.
They released the EP Gate Flowers in 2010.
In 2011, they won the Rookie of the Year award at the Korean Music Awards.
The artist won the Best Rock Song award at the 8th Korean Popular Music Awards in 2011.
The song 'Yebiyok' won the 'Best Rock Song' award.
They gained huge popularity when they appeared in Korean TV show Top Band in 2011.
TOP Band was broadcast on KBS 2TV.
The group reached the semi-finals (top 4) of the competition.
The group reached the Top 4 in KBS 2TV's TOP Band competition.
The group signed a contract with Eco Bridge.
Eco Bridge is the agency of coach Shin Dae-cheol from Top Band.
In 2012, they released their first studio album, Times.
And in 2013 they toured the UK.
In 2014 they released the EP Neulcuen Baemn (늙은 뱀), and in 2015 they disbanded.
The group participated in the MBC I Am a Singer II new singer special episode.
The participation took place in September 2012.
They performed the song 'Mokpo's Tears'.
'Mokpo's Tears' was originally sung by Lee Nan-young.
They achieved a high ranking with this performance.
In 2023, they announced their reunion with new members Lee Insan and Jeon Sangjin.
They released a new single, All In.
They joined the lineup of the DMZ Peace Train Music Festival.

Yun Seung-shik performs as a solo artist under the name Joyeum.
