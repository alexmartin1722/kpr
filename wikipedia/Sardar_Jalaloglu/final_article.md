# Sardar Jalaloglu

Sardar Jalaloglu () (born June 3, 1954) is an Azeri doctor and politician. He was born in the village of Cehri of Babek district of Nakhchivan. He went to school in Ordubad and finished secondary school in Baku. In 1997, he graduated from Azerbaijan State Medical Institute. After graduation, he worked 15 years as a doctor.

== Early life and education ==

Sardar Jalal oglu Mammadov is also known as Sardar Celal oghlu, and his real name is Sərdar Cəlal oğlu Məmmədov.
Serdar Celaloğlu is also known as Serdar Mammadov.
Serdar Celaloğlu was born in Cehri, Babek District, Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic, Azerbaijan SSR.
Serdar Celaloglu comes from a family of teachers.
He studied at the Azerbaijan Medical University between 1970 and 1977.
His postgraduate education was in forensic medicine.
Serdar Celaloglu started working in 1978.
He worked at the Forensic Medicine Expertise Bureau of the Ministry of Health of the Nakhchivan Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic.
Serdar Celaloglu worked as an expert.

In 1985, he joined Azerbaijan National Movement.
He was one of the initiators for establishing Azerbaijan National Movement and till 1991 was member of Nakhchivan National Movement's governing body.
In 1991, he was one of the founders of Azerbaijan Democratic Party, was elected the general secretary of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party in 1994-96, from 1996 to 2005 first deputy chairman of the Party and from 2005 to 2007 deputy chairman of the Party.
He was arrested illegally in the events of 15–16 October 2003, was recognized as a political prisoner by a number of international organizations including European Union and the ECHR and finally was released in March, 2005.
On May 27, 2007 in the Ninth Congress of the ADP, he was elected the chairman of the Party.
Been nominated for 2008 Presidential election from ADP.
One of the founders of newspapers such as: Hurriyyet, Haray and Oyanish.
Author of hundreds of articles, book reviews about Globalization, Philosophy, Monographies in Ethnography, etc. Sardar Jalaloglu been arrested 3 times for his outspoken speeches and political views.
Married, has 4 children.

== Early political activity and Nakhchivan National Movement ==

Serdar Celaloglu's political activity was secret, and he began legal political activity in 1985.
Serdar Celaloglu was a member of the Yurd Public Association.
Sardar Jalaloglu was one of the founders of the Nakhchivan Regional Branch of the Azerbaijan People's Front.
The person was a founder of the Nakhchivan People's Front and a member of the management board of the Nakhchivan People's Front.
He was a member of the Management Board of the Nakhchivan People's Front, a role that lasted from 1988 to 1990.
'Hurriyet' was the organ of the Azerbaijan National Front Nakhchivan branch, was first published in Nakhchivan, and was later published in Baku.

== Founding and early leadership of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party ==

Sardar Jalaloglu co-founded the Initiative Group of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party in Nakhchivan on January 26, 1991, and was selected as the leader of the Initiative Group.
The Founding Congress of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was held on January 26, 1991, and Serdar Celaloğlu was elected as the Chairman of the Founding Committee.
Sardar Jalaloglu led the ideological-political department of that party and assumed this leadership role in 1991.
Sardar Jalaloglu served as the chairman of the Organizational Committee of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party from 1991 to 1994.
Sərdar Cəlaloğlu was the first chairman of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
The First Founding Congress of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was held on May 7–8, 1994, and the first General President of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was elected at this congress.

== Party congresses, mergers, and internal leadership changes ==

The 1st Congress was held on May 7–8, 1994.
The Democratic Party of Azerbaijan held its 3rd (Extraordinary) Congress on March 30, 1996.
The Democratic Party of Azerbaijan held its 4th (Merger) Congress on March 30, 1996.
The second congress of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was held in 1996.
The Justice Party merged with the Azerbaijan Democratic Party at this congress.
The Justice Party officially merged into the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
Ilyas Ismayilov was the chairman of the Justice Party before the merger.
After the merger, Ilyas Ismayilov became the chairman of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
Ilyas Ismayilov was elected as the General Chairman of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
A congress of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was held in 1998.
Ilyas Ismayilov and Rasul Guliyev were elected as co-leaders of the party at this congress.
Sardar Jalaloglu was re-elected as the secretary of the party at this congress.
Rasul Guliyev was a former member of the Justice Party.
Ilyas Ismayilov resigned from the Azerbaijan Democratic Party after the 2000 parliamentary elections.
Rasul Guliyev became the chairman of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
The 8th Congress of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party was held on April 23, 2005.

== Leadership during Rasul Guliyev's absence and internal conflicts ==

Rasul Guliyev was the leader of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party and had been living in the United States since 1996.
Rasul Guliyev was absent from Azerbaijan between 2000 and 2007, during which period Sardar Jalalov spoke on behalf of the party at official meetings.
A return trip for Rasul Guliyev to Azerbaijan was planned before the 2005 parliamentary elections, but while his plane flew as far as Baku, it was not allowed to land and instead landed in Ukraine.
After this incident, Zemina Dunyamaliyeva, the deputy chairman of the Azerbaijan Democratic Party, accused Sardar Jalalov of collaborating with the government and believed that Sardar Jalalov was responsible for the failure of the plan.
Sardar Jalaloglu was elected in 2005.
Conflicts arose within the party around the time of the 2007 parliamentary elections, and two different groups began to operate under the leadership of Rasul Guliyev and Sardar Jalalov.
The faction led by Rasul Guliyev attempted to hold a congress, but this attempt was unsuccessful.
People who supported Rasul Guliyev resigned from the party ranks and established the Open Society Party under the leadership of Rasul Guliyev.
Resul Guliyev was expelled from the party.

== Electoral alliances and parliamentary campaigns ==

The Azerbaijan Democratic Party participated in the 2005 Azerbaijan general elections as a component of the Azadlik Alliance and ran with 36 candidates.
Serdar Celaloğlu was a joint candidate of the Azatlık İttifakı alliance who ran for parliament from the 9th Binegedi Second Electoral District but was not elected.
The person was a founding member of the Karabakh Alliance, which was established during the 2010 Azerbaijan general elections by the Azerbaijan Hope Party and the Azerbaijan Democratic Party.
The person was a joint parliamentary candidate for the alliance.

== Presidential candidacies and election results ==

Serdar Celaloğlu was a presidential candidate in the 2008, 2013, and 2018 Azerbaijani presidential elections.
Sardar Jalaloglu supported Isa Qambari, the unified presidential candidate of Our Azerbaijan Bloc, during the 2003 presidential elections[cite: 1].

He received 22,773 votes, which was 0.62% of the vote share, and finished in last place and 10th place[cite: 2,3].
Sardar Jalalov lost the 2013 presidential election[cite: 2,3].

The Azerbaijan Democratic Party nominated a presidential candidate in the 2018 Azerbaijani presidential election.
The candidate received 119,621 votes, which was 3.03% of the total votes, and finished in third place in the election[cite: 4,5].

== Imprisonment and legal proceedings ==

The arrest occurred during protest actions held after the 2003 presidential elections in Azerbaijan, and the arrests were brief.
Serdar Celaloglu was sentenced to 4 years and 6 months in prison by the Court of Heavy Crimes.
While in prison, Serdar Celaloglu was recognized as a political prisoner by the Council of Europe, and he was released from prison after being recognized as a political prisoner.
Serdar Celaloglu was pardoned in 2005 and released under a general pardon in 2005.
Serdar Celaloglu claimed he was tortured when he was imprisoned and while in custody.
Serdar Celaloglu filed a complaint with the European Court regarding his torture claim and with the European Court of Human Rights.
The European Court of Human Rights concluded that torture cases were not investigated during the trial, and this conclusion was reached on January 11, 2007, when the court issued a ruling.

The ruling ordered the payment of 10,000 euros in compensation, and the investigation involved 10,000 euro compensation payments for the torture cases.
Sardar Jalalov denied the allegations of collaboration with the government.
The imprisonments were of short duration.

== Ideology and views ==

Serdar Celaloglu has a conservative ideology.
Celaloglu argues that men and women are different from creation, asserting that men were created strong and women were created physically weaker.
