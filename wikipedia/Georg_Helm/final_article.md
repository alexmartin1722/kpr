# Georg Helm

Georg Ferdinand Helm (; 15 March 1851 – 13 September 1923) was a German mathematician.

Helm graduated from high school from the Annenschule in Dresden in 1867. Thereafter he studied mathematics and natural sciences at the Dresden Polytechnical School, and then at the universities of Leipzig and Berlin from 1871 to 1873.

Helm first taught at the Annenschule, his high school alma mater. Then he taught mathematics and physics at the Dresden University of Technology and at the Royal Saxon Polytechnic from 1888 until 1922. He was an interdisciplinarian, whose teaching responsibilities included a seminar on insurance statistics. Helm coined the term "mathematical chemistry", and was the first to classify physical and chemical properties as intensive or extensive in 1898.

His work in the area of economics postulated that money was the economic equivalent of the lowest form of social entropy as described in his work "Teachings on Energy" (in German, Lehre von der Energie, 1887).

== Education and career ==

Georg Ferdinand Helm was born in Dresden.
He studied mathematics and natural sciences at the Technical University of Dresden.
He studied under Carl Gottfried Neumann at the University of Leipzig.
Helm taught mathematics and theoretical physics.
He became an ordinary professor in 1892.
He became a professor of applied mathematics starting in 1906.
He served as rector of the Technical University of Dresden.
His tenure as rector was temporary.

== Scientific debates ==

Helm was a representative of Wilhelm Ostwald's Energetics.
In 1895, Helm engaged in a scientific debate with Ludwig Boltzmann as Ostwald's representative at the Natural Scientists' Assembly in Lübeck.
Ludwig Boltzmann was a staunch proponent of atomism and an opponent of Ostwald's views.
Ostwald gave a lecture at the assembly, and Boltzmann focused on his student Helm, having invited Helm and Ostwald specifically to seek a debate with atom critics.
Ernst Mach was not present at the gathering, and the criticism was surprising for Helm.
Boltzmann was the winner of this debate.
Arnold Sommerfeld was present at the assembly and compared the devastating criticism to a bullfight in which the bull represented Boltzmann and the bull won.

== Legacy ==

Georg Helm is buried in the Helm/Zeuner family grave at the Old Annen Cemetery in Dresden, where the engineer Gustav Zeuner is also buried.
The Georg Helm grave is maintained by the Association for the Promotion of Students of the Technical University of Dresden.
Commemoration takes place in a memorial hall for professors of the TU Dresden located on the same cemetery.
The Technical University of Dresden has been awarding a science prize in the university's name since 1995.
Georg Helm is part of the alumni project at TU Dresden.
