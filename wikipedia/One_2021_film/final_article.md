# One 2021 film

One is a 2021 Indian Malayalam-language political drama film directed by Santhosh Vishwanath and written by Bobby & Sanjay. The film stars Mammootty in the lead role. Mathew Thomas, Murali Gopy, Siddique, Joju George, Ishaani Krishna, Gayatri Arun and Nimisha Sajayan plays the supporting roles.

Kadakkal Chandran is the Chief Minister of Kerala and he undergoes a transformation and tries to do good to the populace by bringing Right to Recall (India) tool against corrupt ministers. Will he be able to politically, morally, and sensitively deal with the "plaguing accountability issue" within the existing circumstances becomes the plot.

Affected by the high handedness of police protecting the CM and the challenges faced by commoners when people of power are traveling, Sanal posts an angry anonymous Facebook post lamenting the situation, which goes viral. Opposition parties make this, into a major political issue leading up to a general strike. There is a serious hunt for Sanal by the government and police. The local MLA and attorney are afraid to take up his case. He is apprehended as he tries to flee to Bengaluru. Later the CM declares that he wanted to meet Sanal in person. Though scared, he goes to the official residence of the Chief Minister. On meeting him, Sanal understands that the CM is a good leader and a wonderful human being after the CM reveals the truth behind Sanal's Facebook post. After initial friction, Sanal gets more close to the CM and they become close friends. The CM gives him an appointment and his life takes a new turn.

== Lead roles ==

Jiju George, Nimisha Sajayan, and Shankar Ramakrishnan star in the film.
Jiju George plays the character Murali Gopi as Party Secretary, while Ranjit Selim Kumar plays the character Madhu Shankar Ramakrishnan as Opposition Leader.
Mammootty plays the role of the Chief Minister of Kerala in the film.
Ranjith, Madhu, and Salim Kumar appear as main characters in the film.

== Political and administrative characters ==

Jijo George is the opposition leader, while Babichan and Siddeeq serve as party secretaries.
Mathew Thomas holds the position of Speaker.
Shankar Ramakrishnan is an Advocate General, and Shamsudeen IAS and Sudev Nair are Chief Secretaries.
Noorudeen Suresh Krishna is a councilor, and Karyavattom Sree Kumar Jayakrishnan and Tom Mukadan Nishanth Sagar are Members of the Legislative Assembly.

== Actors in the film ==

Ranjith, Salim Kumar, Balachandran Menon, Alencier, Shankar Ramakrishnan, Shyam Prasad, Ramya, Suresh Krishna, Sudeep Nair, and Sudheer Karamana are actors in the film.
Mammootty plays the character Chandran Kadakkal.

Principal photography began in the month, October 2019.
Sreelakshmi A.R. produced the film under Ichaasis Productions.
Vaidhi Somasundar handled the cinematography of the film and operated the camera for this film.
Thiruvananthapuram was the main location for the film.
The film's shooting took place around the Palayam University Office on November 20, 2019.
News spread that Mammooty had arrived for shooting.
Crowds gathered in Palayam to see the actor.
This gathering became a news story.
The first look poster of the movie was released on November 10, 2019.
The poster was released through Mammooty's own Facebook page.
The film's official teaser was released on February 2, 2020.
The film's second teaser was released on March 7, 2020.

Gopi Sundar is the music director of the movie and composed the background score for this film.
The film has one song, "Janamanssin", a political anthem, sung by Shankar Mahadevan.
Its lyrics were written by Rafeeq Ahamed.

The film was originally scheduled to be released on 22 May 2020, but was postponed due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
It was released on 26 March 2021.
Aan Mega Media distributed the film in theaters.

Following the theatrical release, the film was released on 27 April 2021 on Netflix.

The film received positive reviews from critics.

Sify gave it a rating of 4.5 stars out of 5 and wrote, "A relevant political drama ". Indian Express gave it a rating of 3.5 stars out of 5 and wrote, "Mammootty's political drama is pleasant despite its problems". Times of India gave it a rating of 3 stars out if 5 and wrote, "The portrait of a too-good-to-be-true CM ". Baradwaj Rangan of Film Companion South wrote "The film's problem is in this utter predictability. The chief minister is so good, so pure and selfless that he becomes a big bore. It's easier to portray Ravana on screen than Rama."
