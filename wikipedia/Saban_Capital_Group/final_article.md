# Saban Capital Group

Saban Capital Group LLC is an American investment firm based in Los Angeles, California focused on media, entertainment, and communications investments. Formed in 2001 by Haim Saban, Saban Capital Group owns Saban Films, and co-owns Celestial Tiger Entertainment.

== Saban Music Group ==

Saban Music Group was founded in 1980 by music and television producers Haim Saban and Shuki Levy, and was originally named Saban Records. Saban Records was a US subsidiary of Saban International Paris, which is now known as SIP Animation. The company achieved success in France and expanded into the United States in 1983. [42,43] Saban Music Group was founded in 1983. Warner Communications acquired the first music catalog of Saban Records in 1986 for approximately 6 million dollars. A music publisher was established in 2002 and was sold to Bug Music in October 2010. On October 4, 2010, Bug Music acquired the worldwide rights to a catalog that included complete musical themes, tracks, and scores covering more than 90 television series and 100 movies and television specials.

Haim Saban and Shuki Levy founded Saban Music Group, which was originally named Saban Records.
Warner Communications purchased Saban Records' first music catalog in 1986 for 6 million dollars.
Bug Music purchased the worldwide rights to the full catalog of Saban Music Group on October 4, 2010.
The catalog consists of theme songs and soundtracks from more than 90 television series and 100 television films and specials.
