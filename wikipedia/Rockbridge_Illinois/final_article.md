# Rockbridge Illinois

Rockbridge is a village in Greene County, Illinois, United States. The population was 175 at the 2020 census.

Rockbridge is located in southeastern Greene County at (39.272262, -90.203540).
Illinois Route 267 passes along the eastern edge of the village, leading north to Greenfield and south to Medora.
Carrollton, the Greene County seat, is to the northwest via Routes 267 and 108.
