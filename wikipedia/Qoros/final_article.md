# Qoros

Qoros Auto Co., Ltd. was a Chinese automotive manufacturing company headquartered in Shanghai, China. Its principal activity was the design, development, production and sale of passenger cars sold under the Qoros marque.

It was founded as a joint venture between Chery and Israel Corporation in December 2007 under the name Chery Quantum Automotive Corporation (CQAC). The name was changed to Qoros Auto Co., Ltd. in November 2011. In December 2017 Baoneng Group, secured a 51 percent stake in Qoros for 6.6 billion RMB.

The first production model, the Qoros 3, made its first public appearance at the Geneva Motor Show in March 2013 and went on sale in China in November 2013.

Qoros is an invented word.
The Q is intended to represent quality, and the whole name to echo the Greek chorus, a collective voice in plays and music, reflecting the multinational nature of the company.
The brand name Qoros was chosen for export and is reserved for the foreign market.
The company is known as Guan Zhi in China, where the Chinese name is 观致汽车.
The transliteration of the Chinese name is Guan Zhi, which literally means 'Learning and Perfection'.
Guan is Chinese for learning and observing, while Zhi is Chinese for perfection.

Later in January 2019, Kenon Holdings transferred the 12 percent stake of its 24 percent stake in Qoros to Baoneng Investment Group for RMB 1.56 billion yuan ($230 million).
Baoneng owns 63 percent of Qoros, Kenon Holdings owns 12 percent of Qoros, and Chery Automobile continues to own 25 percent after the deal.

Changan sold its 50-percent share in the joint venture with PSA to Baoneng for 1.63 billion yuan ($234 million).

By September 2020, Qoros 7, the first model of Qoros under Baoneng Investment Group's control was launched.

== Facilities and production capacity ==

The factory for Chery Quantum was located in Wuhu, Anhui, which is the same location as Chery Automobile's headquarters, and was expected to be completed in 2009.
The company's operations center is located in Shanghai, while it maintains design centers, technology centers, and engineering and R&D centers in both Munich and Shanghai.
The company's maximum production capacity will reach 300,000 vehicles per year, and this production capacity can grow to 450,000 cars per year in 2016.

== Technology and brand strategy ==

Qoros did not use Chery's technology or platform, developed its technology platforms in cooperation with suppliers, and directly purchased required technology platforms from suppliers.
Qoros claimed that shareholders only appear as shareholders and do not directly influence corporate decision-making.

== Financial difficulties and restructuring ==

Cost-cutting measures were planned.
In 2017, shareholders attempted to sell their shares, but the attempts failed because no interested party was found.
One reason for the lack of interest in Qoros was its financial losses, as the company accumulated losses up to 2016 totaling around one billion euros.
In January, both shareholders had 72 million euros in funds available intended for the development of electric cars.
Kenon's stock price increased by nearly 30% on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange regarding the stake in its loss-making subsidiary.
Shareholders from both parties announced an increase in investment to Qoros of 500 million yuan.

== Sales performance and decline ==

A significant reason for the owners' loss of interest in Qoros was a sales decline that occurred during the first seven months of 2017, when approximately 40% fewer cars were sold compared to the already weak previous year.
The sales decline continued throughout the year, and as of October 2017, sales were down by 44% and approximately 8,000 vehicles compared to the previous year.

== 2018 growth and fleet orders ==

Qoros achieved growth in the first half of 2018, driven by a large fleet order from LYD, a carsharing startup based in Shenzhen, for 30,524 units.

== Cumulative sales and peak year ==

Qoros sold a total of 164,600 new cars in the People's Republic of China during the period from 2014 to 2022.
The most successful year was 2018, when 62,252 units were sold.

Sales in China commenced in December 2013, and by the first four months of 2014 the company had sold 1,490 Qoros 3 cars.
The sedan is to be joined by a hatchback version in July 2014.
By August 2014, Qoros had sold 2,540 cars.
Qoros is not expected to make a profit in the early years, "as is usual for beginning companies."

The total sales for 2014, the company's first year of operation, was under 7,000 cars, far below its planned capacity for 150,000 cars.
This sales volume represented a market share of approximately 0.3%.
This led to a change in senior management, including the chairman Guo Qian replaced by Chery deputy president and Jaguar Land Rover board chairman Chen Anning.
In February 2015, the company announced a new CEO, former General Motors' executive Phil Murtaugh.
Murtaugh left the company after less than a year.

Over 2015, Qoros sold 14,000 cars.
Production capacity was not nearly fully utilized in 2015.

In 2016, sales of Qoros cars increased by 70 percent, totalling 24,188 for the year, a number that represented an increase of over 400 percent compared to the previous year.

The company had planned to sell models in Europe, and piloted sales in Slovakia, but in April 2017 it formally announced that it had no plans to sell cars in Europe for the foreseeable future.
