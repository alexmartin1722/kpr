# Ferdausi Rahman

Ferdausi Rahman (; born 28 June 1941) also known as Ferdausi Begum is a Bangladeshi playback singer. She is the daughter of folk singer Abbas Uddin. She sang some popular film songs for Pakistani films also in the 1960s especially working with the music composer Robin Ghosh in film Chakori released in 1967. She was awarded Ekushey Padak in 1977 and Independence Day Award in 1995 by the Government of Bangladesh. In addition, she was awarded the lifetime achievement award of the National Film Award in the year 2015.

Rahman had two elder brothers, Justice Mustafa Kamal (died 2015) and singer Mustafa Zaman Abbasi.
Her niece, Nashid Kamal, is a singer.
Her other nieces who are also singers are Samira Abbasi and Sharmini Abbasi.
Ferdousi Rahman was born in Kochbihar, British India.
Rahman married Rezaur Rahman, a mechanical engineer and industrialist, on October 26, 1966, at Shahbagh Hotel.
Rezaur Rahman owned a filter manufacturing industry that was the only filter manufacturing facility in the country.
The factory was sold after the sons moved abroad.
Rezaur Rahman is currently retired.
They have two sons, Rubaiyat Rahman and Razin Rahman.
Rubaiyat Rahman lives in Dallas, United States, and works for Facebook.
Razin Rahman lives in London.

Ferdousi Rahman received training from Ustad Mohammad Hossein Khosrow, Yusuf Khan Qureshi, Kader Jamiri, and Gool Mohammad Khan.
Ferdousi Rahman learned music from his father during his childhood.
Ferdousi Rahman began performing on stage at a very young age and participated in the radio program Khelaghore at the age of 8.
Ferdousi Rahman performed on the Metro stage on June 26, 1956.
Rahman participated as a children artiste in radio programs.
In 1955, she first sang for the radio as an adult artiste.
He recorded his first song from HMV in 1957.
The first released movie where she sang as a playback singer was Ehtesham's Ei Desh Tomar Amar under the music direction of Khan Ataur Rahman in 1959, followed by Asiya in 1960.
One of the music directors of the film Asiya was her father Abbas Uddin.
In 1960, she directed music for the film 'Rajdhani'r Buke' alongside Robin Ghosh.
He sang at the inaugural program of Bangladesh Television on December 25, 1964, at 7 PM.
In 1964, her song was broadcast on the newly established Pakistan Television in erstwhile East Pakistan (now Bangladesh).
He traveled to Russia and China in 1963 and 1966.
Ferdausi Rahman completed a six-month staff notation course on music at Trinity College of Music in London.
He started the song program 'Eso Gaan Shikhi' on December 27.
She used to direct the program 'Eso Gaan Shikhi' on Bangladesh Television.
Her children's program Esho Gaan Shikhi is a program to teach children about music on Bangladesh Television and the program has been running for 44 years.
She is known as Gaaner Khalamoni (Aunt of Song) for this program.
Approximately 5,000 song recordings have been made by her.
She is the first female music director in Bangladesh.
NTV has been airing a program of her songs presented by contemporary artistes.

He received the President Ziaur Rahman Award.

He received a Television Award in 1975.
He received the Bangladesh Film Journalists Association Award in 1976.

Ferdausi Rahman has sung various genres including folk songs, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Sangeet, and modern songs.
