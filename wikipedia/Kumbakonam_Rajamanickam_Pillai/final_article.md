# Kumbakonam Rajamanickam Pillai

Kumbakonam Rajamanickam Pillai (), 5 August 1898 - 1970 was a Carnatic music violinist of Tamil Nadu, Southern India.

He was born in the small village of Alangudi in a Devadasi family near Needamangalam located on the route going from Thanjavur to Tiruvarur.
His mother Kamalathammal was keen that he should develop his musical talents.

He commenced his training first as a vocalist under Nadaswaram maestro Kandaswamy Pillai.
Later on, he underwent further training from Tiruvisanallur Pallavi Narayanaswamy Iyer and then from Pandanallur Chinnaswamy Pillai.
As an instrumentalist he trained in violin for four years under Tirukodikaval Ramaswamy Iyer who was a disciple of violin maestro Tirukodikaval Krishna Iyer.
He learned the Vayalpaattu style from his teacher.
His teachers opined that his music talents were more suited to be a violinist than to be a singer.
Teachers advised that the violin was more suitable than the veena for his musical talent.
He stopped playing the veena.
So, he devoted full concentration as a violinist.

He concentrated on classical Carnatic music.
Most of the time he performed as an accompanist to Carnatic singers rather than giving solo concerts.
He accompanied top singers of his time.
He never played sudden sounds on the violin and never engaged in showing off tricks.
When playing accompaniment, his violin music did not overshadow the vocalist's voice and blended with the vocalist's voice.
His primary concern was the success of the concert, and he preferred not to show off his presence.
He has a simple and calm appearance on stage.

He was honoured in the Royal Courts of Ramanathapuram, Kochchin, Ettayapuram, Trivandrum and Mysore.
He was appointed Asthana Vidhwan in the Courts at Trivancore in 1940 at the Travancore Palace and at Ettayapuram in 1942 at the Ettayapuram Palace.
Trivandrum Chiththirai Thirunal Maharaja honoured Rajamanickam Pillai and presented an elephant to him.

Sangeetha Kalanidhi, 1948 Awarded by Music Academy, Chennai.
Sangeet Natak Akademi, 1959 Isai Perarignar, 1957 Awarded by Tamil Isai Sangam.
The Tamil Music Association in Chennai presented the Isai Parigalarchi award.
The artist received the Music Maestro Award.

M. M. Dandapani Desikar and Mayavaram V. R. Govindaraja Pillai are two of his disciples who became famous. Cine actor Thyagu is his grandson.

== Death ==

Kumbakonam Rajamanickam Pillai died in November 1970.

== Legacy and compositions ==

He donated an elephant dedicated to the Adhi Kumbeswarar Temple.
The piece titled "Sangeetha Gnanamu Keerthana" is in the Tanjasi raga.
The event took place at Alankudi.
