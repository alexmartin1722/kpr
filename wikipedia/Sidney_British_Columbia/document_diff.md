+== History ==

+Captain Clerke, a British Royal Navy captain, led a fleet that explored the area between 1846 and 1848.
+The Fraser Canyon Gold Rush began in 1858, and large numbers of European colonists arrived on Vancouver Island and purchased large amounts of land in the areas now known as Sidney and North Saanich.
+In 1891, the Bretherton family subdivided their land into multiple lots.
+By the end of 1892, Sydney had a grocery store, a post office, and a hotel.
