# Iskenderun Technical University

Iskenderun Technical University (Turkish İskenderun Teknik Üniversitesi, commonly shortened to as İSTE) is a public university located in İskenderun district of Hatay, Turkey. It was formed on March 31, 2015 by the separation from Mustafa Kemal University.

It is a technical university dedicated to engineering sciences as well as provides education in social sciences and music. As of 2021, the university has seven faculties, one college, seven institutes, five vocational schools and one music school.

Iskenderun Technical University was founded with the Law No.
6640 published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Turkey on March 31, 2015.
The Turkish Grand National Assembly passed Law No.
6640 on March 31, 2015.
Most faculties that formed Iskenderun Technical University were separated from Mustafa Kemal University.
These faculties were attached to the new university as a result of this separation.
Tolga Depci was appointed as rector on September 23, 2020.
As of 2021, it is one of the two universities in Hatay.
