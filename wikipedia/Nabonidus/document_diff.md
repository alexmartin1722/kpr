+Private documents were dated according to the king's regnal years, and there are thousands of such private documents.
+Historians consider the Nabonid Chronicles and private documents as the most reliable sources for specific details about his reign.
+This information comes from texts hostile to Nabonid.
+The information provided by Greek historians is of minor significance compared to cuneiform sources.
+
+== Royal self-representation ==
+
+Nabonid shaped his records of activity in the form of self-representations, which were traditionally positive.
