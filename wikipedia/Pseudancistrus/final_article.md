# Pseudancistrus

Pseudancistrus is a genus of suckermouth armored catfishes native to South America.

Pseudancistrus is a genus of ray-finned fish in the tribe Ancistrini of the subfamily Hypostominae of the family Loricariidae, known as armored catfishes, of the order Siluriformes.
It was described by Pieter Bleeker in 1862, and redescribed by Jonathan Armbruster in 2004.
Currently, there are 19 recognized species distributed in northern South America, with the inclusion of the genera Lithoxancistrus, the species formerly known as Hemiancistrus megacephalus, as well as the recently described Pseudancistrus corantijniensis.
These species belong to the order Siluriformes and the family Loricariidae.
Pseudancistrus kwinti belongs to the order Siluriformes and the family Loricariidae.
There is no single morphological feature which distinguishes the group from related genera.
Despite this, Pseudancistrus is generally recognized as a monophyletic group.

Within the genus there is what is called the P. barbatus group.
This group comprises Guyanese derived Pseudancistrus bearing hypertrophied odontodes along the snout and nonevertible cheek plates.
It includes the five species P. barbatus, P. corantijniensis, P. depressus, P. nigrescens, and P. zawadzkii.

== Etymology ==

The scientific name is derived from the word meaning 'false' and the word meaning 'hook'.

== Size and Body Shape ==

The body is robust and elongated.
Representatives of this genus have a total length ranging from 8 to 20 cm.
The head is large.

== Head and Sensory Organs ==

The head is large, and the snout is strongly elongated and flattened on top.
The eyes are small and located on the upper part of the head.
The species has whiskers arranged in branched bundles that emerge from the sides of the head.

== Armor and Plating ==

Bony plates are present on the back and partially on the belly, while they are absent on the sides.

== Fin Morphology ==

The dorsal fin is high, wide, and quite long.
The pectoral fins are long and narrowed, and the spines on the pectoral fins have odontodes in males.
The pelvic fins are moderately wide and have rounded tips.
The adipose fin is small.
The anal fin is small and has a short base.
The caudal fin is moderately wide.

The adipose fin is tiny, the anal fin is small with a short base, and the caudal fin is moderately wide.

== Coloration ==

The coloration is sometimes sandy-yellow and the fins are the same color as the body.

== Ecology ==

The fish feed on soft algae and insect larvae.
They are primarily active at dusk and at night.

== Aquarium trade ==

Pseudancistrus are found in aquariums.
