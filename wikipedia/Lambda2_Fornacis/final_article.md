# Lambda2 Fornacis

{{DISPLAYTITLE:Lambda² Fornacis}}

λ² Fornacis, Latinized as Lambda² Fornacis, is the primary of a binary star system in the southern constellation of Fornax. It is just visible to the naked eye as a dim, yellow-hued point of light with an apparent visual magnitude of 5.78. It is located 83 light years distant from the Sun, based on stellar parallax, and is drifting further away with a radial velocity of +12 km/s.

This object is a G-type main-sequence star with a stellar classification of G1V. It is considered a solar analog, being photometrically-similar to the Sun. The star is an estimated 4.3 billion years old with 1.2 times the mass of the Sun and 1.5 times the Sun's radius. It is radiating three times the luminosity of the Sun from its photosphere at an effective temperature of 5,936 K. The abundance of elements with more mass than helium is 55% higher than in the Sun.

There is a faint co-moving companion star located to the east of the main star at an angular separation of . This is most likely an M5–M6 class red dwarf with 0.11 times the Sun's mass. The projected separation between the pair is about .

== Observation and location ==

Lambda² Fornacis is also known as 36 Fornacis and is abbreviated as Lambda² For.
The star has a right ascension of 02h 36m 58.62s and a declination of -34° 34′ 38.4″.
Lambda² Fornacis is situated in the middle part of the constellation and is visible to the naked eye only in areas without light pollution.
The absolute magnitude of the star is 3.74.
Parallax measurements were conducted within the Hipparcos mission, yielding a measured parallax angle of approximately 38.8 milliarcseconds.
The star is calculated to be located at a distance of approximately 26 parsecs from the Sun.

Precision Doppler spectroscopy from an intensive 48 night observing campaign on the Anglo-Australian Telescope has revealed the presence of a low-mass extrasolar planet orbiting the star.
Astronomers conducted observations in 2009.
The existence of the exoplanet was known since 2009.
HD 16417 b is an exoplanet.
HD 16417 b orbits the star Lambda2 Fornacis.
This object has an orbital period of 17.24 days and an eccentricity of 0.2.
The exoplanet orbits at an average distance of 0.14 AU from its star.
This distance is 36% of the distance between Mercury and the Sun.
It is possible that part of the atmosphere evaporates into space.
The evaporation is caused by the radiation from the star.
This process forms a comet-like tail.

== Stellar properties ==

Lambda2 Fornacis rotates with a projected rotational velocity of 2.1 km/s.
The size and surface gravity of Lambda2 Fornacis are less than 4.5.
The iron-to-hydrogen ratio ([Fe/H]) of Lambda2 Fornacis is +0.13.
Other elements are also overabundant, including nickel, magnesium, and silicon.
The star is 300 million years younger than the Sun.

== Designations ==

Lambda² Fornacis is also known as HD 16417 in astronomical literature, a name given to the star in the Henry Draper Catalogue.
