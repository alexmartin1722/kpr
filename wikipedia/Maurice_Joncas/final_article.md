# Maurice Joncas

Maurice Joncas (26 June 1936 – 13 June 2021) was a Canadian writer.

Maurice Joncas was born in Pointe-Jaune, which is located in Gaspésie.
In 1968, Joncas began participating in numerous shows as a director, actor, and producer.
The following year, he was a founding member of the Théâtre de l'Astran de Gaspé.
A multidisciplinary artist known throughout the Gaspé Peninsula, he also practiced singing, painting, and participated in choirs.
He made a career as a secondary school French teacher for 35 years and retired in 1996.
Maurice Joncas died in Gaspé on 13 June 2021 at the age of 84.

He has authored novels.
