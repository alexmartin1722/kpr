# Michael Chiesa

Michael Keith Chiesa (; born December 7, 1987) is an American professional mixed martial artist and sports analyst who competes in the welterweight division in the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC). A professional mixed martial artist since 2008, Chiesa was the winner of FX's inaugural The Ultimate Fighter: Live.

== Fighting style ==

Chiesa's fighting style is a mix of wrestling and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
His wrestling background helped him take fights to the ground, get his opponents' backs, and engage in ground fighting.
This background also allowed him to escape difficult situations against other wrestling styles.
Chiesa has benefited greatly in chokes.
When reaching the opponent's back, he has a specific habit of performing a body triangle using his legs instead of a traditional Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu technique.
He hooks the opponent's legs to lock them in place.
This lock most often leads to a rear-naked choke.
His long limbs allow him to tighten chokes and other holds more effectively.
