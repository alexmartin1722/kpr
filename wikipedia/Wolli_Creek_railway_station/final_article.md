# Wolli Creek railway station

Wolli Creek railway station is located at the junction of the Airport and Illawarra lines, serving the Sydney suburb of Wolli Creek. It is served by Sydney Trains T4 Illawarra line, T8 Airport & South line services and NSW TrainLink South Coast line services.

Wolli Creek station was built as part of the Airport line opening on 21 May 2000.
The name derives from a local creek branching from the Cooks River, and Wolli Junction is the name of the original junction point for the East Hills line from the Illawarra line at this site.
In the initial concept for the Airport Line, no station was to be provided at this site, however the State Rail Authority decided to construct a station here as an interchange point with the new Airport line and the Illawarra line.
The state railway authority made a decision in 1996 to transform the location into an interchange station.
The station was built to facilitate travel for suburban residents and to facilitate travel to the airport.
The station, unlike the others built on the line, was constructed with government finance and hence is owned by the Transport Asset Holding Entity.
Wolli Creek Station is the only Airport & South Line station managed by the government, while other stations on the Airport & South Line are managed by Airport Link.
In the initial concept and design phase, the station was named North Arncliffe, however this was changed to Wolli Creek in 1998.
During construction, a set of points allowing the cross-over of trains from the Illawarra local lines to the Illawarra main lines was moved further south.
A secondary motivation was to promote urban renewal around the station site.
The station is located on a former industrial site and siding, and the area around the station was occupied by light industrial businesses when the station opened.
Since the station's opening, the suburb has been significantly redeveloped with high density housing which as of 2015 is ongoing.
As part of this development, the Airport line platforms have been partially covered over.
The railway company planned to add platforms, and the original Airport Line platform was later constructed into its current form.

== Incidents ==

On April 9, 2008, a train on the Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line suddenly smoked, causing train services to be delayed by approximately 40 minutes.
The fire was extinguished at 8:30 local time.

== Station layout and levels ==

The station has three levels with the concourse located on the middle level.
The Illawarra line is on the upper level, while the Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line and the East Hills and Airport lines are on the lower level.
The platforms of the three lines are arranged in a cross shape, with platforms 3 and 4 serving the Illawarra Line and platforms 1 and 2 serving the Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line.
The concourse is situated above the East Hills and Airport Line platforms, and the station has an open plan with no separation between the concourse and platform areas.
The platforms for the Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line are in a semi-underground state and are located in a pit below ground level.
The Illawarra line has four tracks, but only one island platform could be built among them, resulting in another set of Illawarra line tracks not having a platform.
The station is still located next to platform 3 today.
Platforms for the Eastern Suburbs and Airport lines were built on the lower level of the station.

== Concourse and passenger access ==

The station has facilities for people with disabilities and an elevator.
A transfer concourse is located in the middle, and passengers must pass through this concourse to transfer trains.
Passengers entering or exiting the Eastern Suburbs and Airport lines can reach the concourse by taking an elevator or climbing stairs.
Passengers using the Illawarra line must walk through an additional tunnel section to reach the concourse, where an elevator is located at the end of the tunnel connecting the Illawarra line platform to the tunnel.
Electronic boards are located in the concourse and in the tunnel.
The south side of the concourse is the only entrance and exit to the station, which does not have ticket gates.

== Ticketing facilities ==

A ticket window and an automatic ticket machine are located outside the exit.
The ticket office is equipped with a hearing aid.

== Line services and destinations ==

The station is a transfer station for the Illawarra Line and the Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line, where the latter is labeled only as the 'Illawarra Line' and train services for both lines are shared.
All trains heading to Sydney Airport must pass through this station and depart from Platform 1, while Platform 2 serves trains heading to Macquarie.
The destination of this line is City Hall, and the Eastern Suburbs and Illawarra Line has a joint service to Bondi and goes to Waterfall and Cronulla.
The South Coast Line has a joint service to Sydney Central or Bondi, though the Eastern Suburbs Line does not have a range at this station.
Passengers must transfer themselves at this station to go to Bondi Junction.

== Stopping patterns and operations ==

During peak hours, some Eastern Suburbs Line trains pass through this station to reach Central Station without stopping.
Almost all Illawarra Line and South Coast Line trains stop at this station during peak hours, while all trains on both lines stop during off-peak hours.
The joint service on the South Coast Line is limited to peak hours.

Transit Systems operates one bus route from Wolli Creek station, under contract to Transport for NSW: 348: to Prince of Wales Hospital.
In April 2009, Sydney Bus extended route 348 from Alexandria to this station.
Passengers must transfer to a night bus.
The night bus travels along Prince's Highway.
