+== Architecture ==
+
+The facade of the Church of Santa Maria Podone is in the Baroque style and has a gable roof design.
+The facade is crowned by a triangular pediment containing the coat of arms of the Borromeo family.
+There is a large window above the facade, and each pair of pilasters has a rectangular window with a marble frame at its center.
+The church has a neoclassical portico supported by four marble columns.
+The portico houses the ancient entrance portal to the church, which was donated by the chaplain Ambrogio di Pietro and created by Samuele and Policleto Luvoni.
+The bell tower dates from the 16th century and was designed by Pellegrino Tibaldi.
+The chapel was added by the Borromeo family.
+
+== Interior ==
+
+The church interior originally had three naves, each ending with a semicircular apse, and this structural layout remains intact.
