+At the beginning of June, 29 people were arrested in Urumqi on suspicion of terrorism, inciting separatism, and ethnic hatred.
+Police in Shache County, Xinjiang discovered five bombs placed at different locations, and the remaining bombs were removed in time.
+On May 24, residents of Celer County reported to the public security organs that a person named A had previously promoted and incited ethnic hatred among others.
+Police officers quickly went to the scene along with village cadres and resident cadres.
+During the identity check process, five individuals shouted slogans calling for holy war and attacked police officers with knives.
+Police officers killed three of the attackers on the spot.
+The police involved were from the Luntai County Public Security Bureau, and the action was based on reports from the public.
+The suspects were involved in illegal gatherings and holding illegal religious activities.
+A large quantity of physical training equipment and books containing religious extremist content were seized.
+Law enforcement agencies in Lop County and Qiemo County seized prohibited items such as controlled knives and blank ammunition for starting guns based on public reports.
+
+Some members of the public voluntarily surrendered prohibited items including hunting rifle bullets, detonators, fuses, and replica firearms.
+As of June 13, public security organs across Xinjiang received more than 300 tips from the public.
+Public security organs busted 9 violent and terrorist gangs and 1 religious extremist gang.
+More than 60 people involved in these cases were arrested.
+More than 160 explosive devices were seized, more than 40 kilograms of raw materials for making explosives were discovered, a batch of tools for making explosives was seized, multimedia cards were seized, and illegal religious propaganda items such as 'Jihad' flags were seized.
+Two illegal audio-video recording points were dismantled.

+== Trial and sentencing ==
+
+The individuals were convicted of terrorism, separatism, and murder.
+Three of the defendants were sentenced to death.
+The trial took place at an open-air stadium in the Ili-Kazakh Autonomous Prefecture.
+Seven thousand people were present at the trial.
+The defendants were wearing orange vests, surrounded by armed guards, standing with their heads bowed inside the beds of trucks.
+The individuals sentenced to death killed several members of one family in 2013.
+According to authorities, the killings were carried out with extreme cruelty.
+Nine people were sentenced to death on June 5.
+Three people were sentenced to the death penalty with a two-year reprieve.
+Eighty-one people were sentenced.
+All 81 sentenced individuals were residents of the Xinjiang-Uyghur Autonomous Region.
+The cases were heard in six city courts.
+Judgments were issued for 23 cases.
+
+Defendants were charged with preparing terrorist attacks, participating in extremist organizations, arson, and murder.
+The court selected various prison terms for the defendants that ranged up to life imprisonment, though details of the sentences were not provided.
+The High People's Court of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region organized a press conference to announce the public sentencing results of 16 cases involving crimes related to spreading violent and terrorist audio-video materials.
+The charges in these 16 cases included organizing, leading, or participating in a terrorist organization, inciting ethnic hatred or discrimination, and illegally manufacturing firearms.
+Thirty-nine defendants involved in these cases were sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment according to law, with the longest prison term being 15 years.
+The group purchased vehicles to be used in the crime and manufactured explosive devices.
+The Urumqi Intermediate People's Court held a first-instance trial on December 8.
+The court determined that the criminal suspect organization was formed in February 2014, purchased vehicles and explosive materials, and manufactured explosives.
+
+The organization tested explosives and one of the small bombs exploded.
+Muhaimiti Yibirehemu was sentenced to life imprisonment and deprived of political rights for life.
+Abulizi Dawuti and Nurahemaiti Abulipiz were sentenced to death, bringing the total number of people sentenced to death to six.
+Ruzaimaiti Wubuli'ershan was sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve and deprived of political rights for life.
+Abudilimati Mahemuti and two others were sentenced to 10 years in prison and deprived of political rights for three years.
