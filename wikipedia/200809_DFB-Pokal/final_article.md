# 200809 DFB-Pokal

The 2008–09 DFB-Pokal was the 66th season of the annual German football cup competition. The competition began with the first round on 7 August 2008, and ended with Werder Bremen defeating Bayer Leverkusen, who for their part eliminated defending champions Bayern Munich in the quarter-finals, in the final at the Olympiastadion, Berlin on 30 May 2009. The winners of the 2008–09 DFB-Pokal would qualify to the fourth qualifying round of the 2009–10 UEFA Europa League.

Due to a decision made in 2006, reserve teams from professional clubs are no longer allowed to compete.

== Background ==

The DFB-Pokal is the most important football cup tournament in Germany and is organized by the German Football Association.
The 2008–09 DFB-Pokal season was for men's football.

== Tournament records and history ==

This was the first time a team won the competition playing exclusively away matches throughout the tournament.
VfL Wolfsburg reached the final in 1994/95 as a second-division team and reached the final only through away game successes before losing the final.
This final was contested for the first time after 13 years, with the previous final taking place in the 1995/96 season.
Both teams had not qualified for an international competition via the league or the European Cup, and neither team qualified through the league.

== Club achievements ==

This victory marked Werder Bremen's fifth national cup title and was the sixth time in the club's history that they won the trophy.
This victory marked SV Werder Bremen's sixth cup title, and Werder won the German Cup for the sixth time.
FC Bayern München was the record holder, having won the cup 14 times previously.
Bayer 04 Leverkusen were finalists in 2002, while SV Werder Bremen were finalists three times in 1989, 1990, and 2000.
