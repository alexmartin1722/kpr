# 202021 Danish Cup

The 2020–21 Danish Cup, also known as Sydbank Pokalen, was the 67th season of the Danish Cup competition.

Randers defeated SønderjyskE in the final of the competition on 13 May 2021. As the winner of the tournament, Randers earned qualification into the play-off round of the 2021–22 UEFA Europa League.

== Overview ==

The tournament was organized by the Danish Football Association.
SønderjyskE Fodbold was the defending champion.

Assuming a similar structure to 2019–20, 92 teams are expected to participate in the first round, coming from all levels of competition.
Six additional teams joined in the second round, while the top six teams from the 2019–20 Danish Superliga entered in the third round.
Matches were played in a single game until the fourth round.
