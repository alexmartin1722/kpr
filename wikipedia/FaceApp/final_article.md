# FaceApp

FaceApp is a photo and video editing application for iOS and Android developed by FaceApp Technology Limited, a company based in Cyprus. The app generates highly realistic transformations of human faces in photographs by using neural networks based on artificial intelligence. The app can transform a face to make it smile, look younger, look older, or change gender.

== Development and company ==

FaceApp was developed by Wireless Lab, which is headquartered in Saint Petersburg, Russia, and is based in Russia.
The subject is classified as social media software and as deep learning software.
Yaroslav Goncharov is the executive director of FaceApp Technology Limited.

FaceApp was launched on iOS in January 2017 and on Android in February 2017.
FaceApp has a free version and a pro version.
There are multiple options to manipulate the photo uploaded such as editor options of adding an impression, make-up, smiles, hair colors, hairstyles, glasses, age or beards.
Filters, lens blur and backgrounds along with overlays, tattoos, and vignettes are also a part of the app.
Wetness effects are part of the application.
The filter was added in April 2017.
FaceApp can use photos from the user's gallery and allows users to take a photo within the app.
One option is a skin filter.
The app allows users to add bangs to photos and to add mustaches to photos.
Examples of available filters include color adjustment.
FaceApp Technology Limited decided to remove the Face Swap option in May 2023.
The gender change transformations of FaceApp have attracted particular interest from the LGBT and transgender communities, due to their ability to realistically simulate the appearance of a person as the opposite gender.

== Popularity and rankings ==

Fast Company ranked the app among the top 25 fastest-growing apps in the world.
The app gained widespread popularity worldwide.

Following these media appearances, more users in Japan started uploading photos edited with this function to social media for enjoyment.

FaceApp re-entered the top popular apps list.

The program ranked first in downloads in most countries.

The program was ranked first in Cambodia, Bhutan, China, Hong Kong, Japan, Laos, Madagascar, Macedonia, Mongolia, and Korea on July 17.

The program was ranked first in Thailand, Vietnam, and Zimbabwe on July 17.

== Business model ==

The app is free to download and includes a 3-day trial period.
Users must cancel within the trial period to avoid an automatic annual charge of 3,200 yen that occurs after the trial ends.
There were critical voices regarding the requirement to cancel within the trial period.
The relevant part of the aforementioned regulations was later revised.
FaceApp earned more than $1 million over a 10-day period of peak demand.

In 2019, FaceApp attracted criticism in both the press and on social media over the privacy of user data.
Among the concerns raised were allegations that FaceApp stored users' photos on their servers, and that their terms of use allowed them to use users' likenesses and photos for commercial purposes.
In response to questions, the company's founder, Yaroslav Goncharov, stated that user data and uploaded images were not being transferred to Russia but instead processed on servers running in the Google Cloud Platform and Amazon Web Services.
According to Goncharov, user photos were only stored on servers to save bandwidth when applying multiple filters, and were deleted shortly after being uploaded.
US senator Chuck Schumer expressed "serious concerns regarding both the protection of the data that is being aggregated as well as whether users are aware of who may have access to it" and called for an FBI investigation into the app.

A "hot" transformation was available in the app in 2017 supposedly making its users appear more physically attractive, but this was accused of racism for lightening the skin color of black people and making them look more European.
The feature was briefly renamed "spark" before being removed.
Founder and chief executive Yaroslav Goncharov apologised, describing the situation as "an unfortunate side-effect of the underlying neural network caused by the training set bias, not intended behaviour" and announcing that a "complete fix" was being worked on.
In August that year, FaceApp once again faced criticism when it featured "ethnicity filters" depicting "White", "Black", "Asian", and "Indian".
The filters were immediately removed from the app.

== Data retention and deletion policies ==

Wireless Lab stated that most images are deleted from the servers within two days after the upload date.
The company guarantees that any user can delete their data from the application's server by sending a request beforehand.

== Third-party data sharing and sales ==

The company claims it does not sell user information to third parties and does not share user information with third parties.
Data privacy is not guaranteed by the application.

== Terms of service and user licensing ==

This license permits copying, modification, adaptation, publication, translation, creation of derivative works, distribution, public performance, and display.

== Security threats and malware ==

In 2019, attackers used a fake website that offered a free 'premium' version of FaceApp to install malware on Android devices.
Technology expert Steve Summerino warned that the FaceApp face database could be used to access banking information.
IT security expert Ariel Feuchtwang warned that combining the FaceApp database with databases collected from Facebook and other sources would make the situation worse.

== Government and law enforcement response ==

The FBI responded to this question and stated that applications like FaceApp and similar products are considered a potential threat to counterintelligence activities.
This threat assessment is based on the data collected by the product, the privacy and terms of service policies, and the legal mechanisms available to the Russian government to access data within Russia.
The investigation was prompted by concerns over national security.

== Device access and data collection ==

The application collects structured data such as GPS information.
The program obtains full access to photos, including those the user plans to process and all images on the device, which may include document scans.

== Racism and filter controversy ==

FaceApp faced criticism again on August 9, 2017.
Yaroslav Goncharov removed the filters from the application immediately.
