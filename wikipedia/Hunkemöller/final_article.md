# Hunkemöller

Hunkemöller is a global omnichannel retailer specialising in lingerie and related products. The company was founded in 1886 in Amsterdam as a speciality shop for corsets. Today, it is a brand with more than 7,400 employees in 19 countries. Hunkemöller is a market leader in the Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark and Germany.

Wilhelm Hunkemöller and his wife Josephina Lexis founded the company in 1886 as a special shop for corsets and bustles in Amsterdam.
In the early years, they produced products in their own factory.
In the following years, the family-owned business expanded and opened stores in Amsterdam, Rotterdam and Utrecht.
In 1974 the company was acquired by Vendex subsidiary Confendex B.V., a subsidiary company of Vroom & Dreesmann.
At this point, the company name was changed from "Hunkemöller Lexis" to simply "Hunkemöller".
In 1977 the company expanded to Belgium and ten years later to Germany.
Today, Hunkemöller has stores throughout Europe and franchise partner stores in the Middle East.
In 2003 online operations began in The Netherlands.

== Founders and early operations ==

Wilhelm Hunkemöller was a German immigrant from Warendorf who lived from 1858 to 1927.
Josephina Lexis was from Roermond.
The company was originally specialized in bustiers.
Production started at a factory in Amsterdam.
The couple opened branches in the following year.

== Acquisition by Vroom & Dreesmann ==

After the turbulent 1960s, Hunkemöller decided to expand its business.
At the end of the 1970s, the company decided to expand in the Netherlands and planned to supplement its own branches with several franchise locations.
Expansion was the main goal after the acquisition, and the company became a subsidiary of Vroom & Dreesman B.V.

== International expansion ==

The first Hunkemöller store in Sweden opened in 2011 and was located in Emporia.

== Sale to Maxeda and PAI Partners ==

Hunkemöller was sold to Maxeda, a transaction facilitated by Vendex that took place in 2010.
Maxeda sold the chain, which had more than 400 stores and an annual revenue of 200 million euros, to the investment group PAI Partners in late November 2010.
PAI Partners paid approximately 265 million euros and owned 97% of the business at that time.
The company expanded after the acquisition.

== Products ==

Hunkemöller specializes in women's apparel and primarily sells lingerie and bras.

Hunkemöller operates an omni-channel model: own-operated physical stores, outlets (for clearance), shop-in-shops, digital channels and international franchise units.
Hunkemöller began its franchise program in 1979.
The company was acquired by The Carlyle Group in early 2016.
Since the end of 2010 the company belonged to PAI Partners, a European private equity group.
Before it was part of Maxeda, a retail business from the Netherlands who in 2009 appointed the current CEO Philip Mountford to lead a period of expansion and transformation.
Hunkemöller's headquarters is in Hilversum, the Netherlands.
In 2021 the business operated 900 physical stores, 19 international webshops, has around 7,400 employees and 4.7 million active members.
More than 7,400 employees work in these stores.
Flagship stores are concentrated in Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Vienna, Stuttgart, Oberhausen, Duisburg, and Antwerp.
In October 2021, The Carlyle Group shared its intention for the sale of the business.
In October 2021, CEO Philip Mountford estimated the brand's worth as €1bn (£855.45m).
In 2022, the company was sold for an undisclosed amount to a group of Dutch investing firms.
The Carlyle Group retains a minority of the shares.

== Geographic presence ==

Europe is the main market for Hunkemöller, with Germany being especially a main market.
The brand concentrates the majority of its network in Northern Europe and is well established in Luxembourg.
The region is the Benelux countries.
Hunkemöller has been present in France since 2003 and has 17 points of sale in France.
The brand is present in Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Russia, Poland, and Spain.
The company operates in North Africa and in the Caribbean part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands.

== Flagship stores ==

Flagship stores are located in Munich, Stuttgart, Oberhausen, Amsterdam, Vienna, Antwerp, and Rotterdam.
