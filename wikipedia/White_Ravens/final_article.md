# White Ravens

The White Ravens is a catalog published annually in English by the International Youth Library as a recommendation list for international children's and youth literature. The most recent White Ravens catalog is presented each year in October at the Frankfurt Book Fair and the following spring at the Bologna Children's Book Fair in Italy. The first catalog was issued in 1986.

== International Youth Library ==

The term White Ravens stands for extraordinary and innovative children's and young adult literature.
The International Youth Library was founded in 1949 after World War II and is located in Munich.
The International Youth Library is dedicated exclusively to children's literature.

From the large quantity of the review and donation copies, which the library receives from publishing houses, institutions, organizations and other friends of the library, the language specialists (lektors) select 200 new releases from over 40 countries in more than 30 languages.
The library's collection consists of donations from organizations worldwide.
The White Raven label is given to books which are chosen "based on the universal relevance of the themes they address, their literary and pictorial qualities, or their innovative approaches or design."
Each title listed in the catalog is briefly described with a short annotation and several keywords that capture content and genre, as well as a notation for suggested reader age level.
The catalogs contain a name index, subject index and reading age index.
In earlier editions, identification symbols for "Special Mentions", for books found to contribute to intercultural communication, and for easy-to-read texts were added to some annotations.
Books are classified by language and then by country.
The 2022 selection contains 200 books.
The 2022 selection includes books written in 37 languages.
Philippine, Hindi, Estonian, and Farsi are among the languages represented in the 2022 selection.
The 2022 selection includes books published in 53 countries.
There were 18 French-language books in this edition.
The French-language books were published in Belgium, Canada, France, Madagascar, Switzerland, and Tunisia.

An online database of titles in the catalogs from 2012 to the current year is hosted on the website of the IYL. It includes an advanced search facility by catalog year.

Beginning in 2010 the International Youth Library has organised a biennial week-long programme of readings and book-related events by invited authors and illustrators from Germany and countries around the world.
These take place on the grounds of Blutenburg Castle as well as in schools, libraries and cultural venues throughout the state of Bavaria.
The guest authors have often won awards in their home countries, even if their works have not been selected for presentation in the White Ravens catalog.
The 7th White Ravens Festival was held in July 2023 and included a programme of 13 authors performing multiple readings at over 60 venues.

== Overview and organization ==

The White Ravens Festival is an international literature festival held in Munich, Germany, that focuses on children's and young adult literature and lasts for six days.
Organized by the International Youth Library Foundation, the festival operates under the patronage of the Bavarian State Minister for Science and Art, with its main venue located at the International Youth Library.
The main goals of the festival are to promote reading and to introduce literature from other nations to German children.

== Venues and reach ==

They perform in youth centers, museums, universities, and prisons.
This program is offered in addition to open events.
Approximately 80 events take place during the festival week in 40 cities and municipalities.
Up to 10,000 guests attend the events.

== Event format and language ==

Authors present their texts in their native language at all events, while actors read from the German translation.
Moderators and interpreters guide the conversation between the author and the audience.

== Workshops and discussions ==

Illustrator workshops, workshop discussions, and panel discussions are offered.
The topics of these events concern children's and youth literature.

== 2023 edition ==

The festival ran from July 16 to July 20, 2023.

== Bologna Children's Book Fair ==

The selected works are displayed in paper format at the library's booth during the Bologna Children's Book Fair.
