# Clarks Louisiana

Clarks is a rural village in Caldwell Parish, Louisiana, United States. The population was 1,017 at the 2010 census. Clarks is the largest community by population in Caldwell Parish.

== Government ==

Clarks is a municipality in the United States.

Clarks is located southwest of the center of Caldwell Parish at (32.026445, -92.139516).
U.S. Route 165 passes by the east side of the village, leading northeast to Columbia, the parish seat, and southwest to Olla.
