+== History ==
+
+The Berlin Zoo, the Frankfurt/Main Zoo, and the Cologne Zoo are older than the Dresden Zoo.
+A committee was formed in 1859 at the suggestion of the Dresden 'Poultry Breeding Association' to initiate the founding of a zoo.
+The committee worked with the city administration.
+Carl Adolph Canzler was responsible for all buildings of the zoo, while Peter Joseph Lenné served as the landscape architect.
+The zoo was built over a period of two years and opened on May 9, 1861.
+Albin Schoepf was the first director of the zoo and built it with the help of his son, Adolph Schoepf, who later became a director of the zoo.
+Gustav Brandes led the zoo from 1910 to 1934 and laid the foundations for a modern zoo.
+The zoo was previously structured as a mere animal display but was restructured to provide appropriate housing for animals in a natural environment.
+
+The bombing raid took place on February 13, 1945, during which the Zoological Garden in Dresden suffered severe destruction and almost the entire animal population of the garden was destroyed.
+The Zoological Garden reopened in May 1946.
+Wolfgang Ullrich applied for an assistant position in 1950 and was immediately appointed as director, leading the Zoological Garden until his death in 1973.
+A support association for the zoo was founded in 1992.
