# Were All Errand Boys

We're All Errand Boys (Swedish: Springpojkar är vi allihopa!) is a 1941 Swedish comedy film directed by Ivar Johansson and starring Åke Söderblom, Rune Halvarsson and Gunnar Höglund. It was shot at the Råsunda Studios in Stockholm. The film's sets were designed by the art director Arne Åkermark. Location shooting took place in Stockholm.

Two firms located opposite each other engage in cutthroat rivalry to land a large order.

== Production ==

The film is based on Harry Iseborg's play Sänd gossen.
It was shot between July 5 and September 13, 1940, at Filmstaden in Råsunda and in Klarakvarteren.

== Kitchen boys and their employers ==

Putte is a piccolo at Hotel Centrum.
Åke Söderblom plays Gustav 'Gugge' Svensson, who is a kitchen boy for AB Delikatesser.
Rune Halvarsson plays Nuffe Olsson, who is a kitchen boy for AB Skånedelikatesser.

== Wholesalers and their employers ==

Thor Modéen plays Konrad Andersson, a wholesaler at AB Delikatesser.
Benkt-Åke Benktsson plays Nordin, a wholesaler at AB Skånedelikatesser.

== Other employees and residents ==

Lillan is a café waitress, and Director Johansson works at Landsortens Inköpsförening.
Annie Nordin is the daughter of wholesaler Nordin, and Viran Rydkvist plays a farm girl.
Miss Göransson works at AB Delikatesser, where Walter Lindström is Hammarlund.
Olle Florin is Larsson at AB Skånedelikatesser, Richard Lund is Jonasson at AB Skånedelikatesser, and Georg Skarstedt is Berglund at AB Skånedelikatesser.
Johansson's wife is Astrid Bodin, and Hugo Tranberg is the doorman at Hotel Centrum.

== Competition and event personnel ==

Ragnar Widestedt is the lawyer.
Rolf Botvid and Folke Algotsson are competition officials.
Uno Larsson is a man at the goal.
Gösta Bodin is the parachutist who is to be caught.

== Family members ==

Signe Lundberg-Settergren is Gugg's mother.

== Musical contributors ==

Esten Areschoug, Gunnar Höglund, Åke Johansson, and Lennart Nyberg contributed to the song.

== Release ==

The film premiered on January 4, 1941, is suitable for children, and has been broadcast on SVT.
The film was released on DVD in 2016.

== Music ==

The lyrics for the song were written by Åke Söderblom.
Bert Sorbon, Rune Halvarsson, Lill-Acke Jacobson, Arne Söderberg, Arne Andersson, and Karl-Gustaf Jonsson contributed to the song.
