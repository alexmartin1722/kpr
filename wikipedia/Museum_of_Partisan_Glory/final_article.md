# Museum of Partisan Glory

The Museum of Partisan Glory is an underground museum in Ukraine. It was established in 1969 in the village of Nerubayske in Odesa Oblast. It was built in an abandoned mine that was used in the construction of Odesa. The catacombs are located at a depth of 12–14 meters. During the Great Patriotic War the catacombs in the village hosted one of the largest guerrilla units in the region. For several centuries coquina was quarried here for the construction of Odesa.

The Museum is a monument of the partisan movement in the Odesa region and, in particular, the soldiers of the detachment of the Hero of the Soviet Union Vladimir Molodtsova-Badaeva. Within six months, a 70-person detachment was active in sabotage and intelligence work. Most of them died. Badaevtsev camp is located two kilometers from the main entrance to the catacombs that were flooded by groundwater. However, enthusiasts club researched archival documents and eyewitnesses and restored its original appearance.

== Description ==

The Museum of Partisan Glory is one of the few underground museums in Ukraine and is a military and historical museum.
The museum consists of an above-ground part, while its underground section is located in a small fenced-off part of the catacombs.
The Museum of Partisan Glory is the only official entrance to the catacombs, which are spacious and span several kilometers.
There are other entrances to the catacombs in the village, but these other entrances were filled in due to the frequent disappearances.

== Exhibits ==

According to eyewitness accounts, the unit's base was equipped with a bathhouse, a kitchen, a shooting range, a headquarters, male and female dormitories, rest areas, and weapon storage.
Items of partisan daily life and samples of weapons are displayed in the underground chambers of the memorial complex.
Inscriptions from that time period are present on the walls.
Archival documents and photographs of Odessa partisans are displayed in the above-ground part of the museum.
The exhibition tells about partisan unit commanders and fighter heroes.
One of the museum's stands is dedicated to the famous tourists of the Nerubayivka catacombs.
A letter of thanks from Fidel Castro is displayed among other rarities in the museum.
The exhibitions focus on World War II and partisans of the Odessa region.
Visitors can learn about the daily life of partisans.

== Activities ==

The Museum of Partisan Glory is a traditional meeting place for veterans.
Open lessons for schoolchildren take place there, and lectures on the history of the partisan movement are held for students.

== Additional information ==

People frequently disappeared in the catacombs.
