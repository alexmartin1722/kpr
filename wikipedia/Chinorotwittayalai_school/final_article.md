# Chinorotwittayalai school

Chinorotwittayalai School (also known as Wat Chinorot School) () is a co-education school for grades 7 through 12 in Wat Chinorasaram Woravihara, Thailand.

The school was founded by Somdej Phra Maha Somanachao Krom Phra Poramanuchit Chinoros in 1895.

Wat Chinorasaram Worawihan was built by Somdet Phra Maha Samon Chao Dom Pramoj Anuchit Chinoros.
The princely monk, Somdej Phra Maha Somanachao Krom Phra Poramanuchit Chinoros ordered the construction of the temple in approximately 1836 (at that time Somdej Phra Maha Somanachao Krom Phra Poramanuchit Chinoros already died.
He was born on December 11, 2333, died 2396).
The formal common education system began in 2444 BE (1901 CE).
The Ministry of Religion appointed Chaiwong Taweep as the first headmaster.
Chaiwong Taweep was formerly a monk who had left the monastic order to become a teacher.
There were 101 students at the school.
There were 3 teachers at the school.
In 2450 BE (1907 CE), Mrs.
Man Aknibut built a building along the Mon Canal.
The building was initially used for Buddhist studies at Wat Chinorasaram.
Mrs.
Man Aknibut later donated the building and its management to the Ministry of Religion.
The school was upgraded from elementary levels one and two to the primary school level.
