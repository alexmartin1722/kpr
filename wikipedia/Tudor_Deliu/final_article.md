# Tudor Deliu

Tudor Deliu (29 October 1955 – 4 August 2023) was a Moldovan politician, professor, and lecturer. He was a member of the Parliament of Moldova from 2010 to 2019, on the lists of the Liberal Democratic Party of Moldova, acting as chairperson of the parliamentary faction of PLDM and secretary of the "Parliamentary Commission, Appointments and Immunities".

== Early life and education ==

Tudor Gheorghe Deliu was born in Malkoch, Yaloveni District, in the Moldavian SSR, USSR.
He studied journalism and graduated from the State University of Moldova in 1981.
He was a microbiologist.

During the period from 1990 to 1994, Deliu was mayor of Răzeni village in Ialoveni District.
Tudor Deliu was the first president of the social-political movement "For the Nation and Country Party" (now the Greater Moldova Party) from 5 May 2007 to the summer of 2008, when the party joined the Liberal Democratic Party of Moldova.
Subsequently, Tudor Deliu was on the PLDM electoral lists, but the movement continued to function.
In 2014 he was included in position 52 in the "TOP 100 most influential politicians of November 2014" (from the Republic of Moldova) in the version of Institute for Political Analysis and Consultancy "POLITICON".
On 9 September 2018, at the 7th Congress of the Liberal Democratic Party of Moldova, Tudor Deliu was elected the new party's president.

== Academic career ==

In 1998, he became a lecturer at Academia de Administrare Publică.
In 2006, he obtained a master's degree in constitutional and administrative law from Academia de Administrare Publică.

Deliu was a football fan, played football and even coached a local team.
He was married and has three children.
Besides Romanian, he also spoke French and Russian.
Tudor Deliu was a veteran of the armed conflict on the left bank of the Dniester, where he was the Major General of the Carabinieri.
Tudor Deliu died on 4 August 2023, at the age of 67, in Chisinau, Moldova, due to an illness.
