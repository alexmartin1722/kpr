# 2019 Shymkent Challenger II

The 2019 Shymkent Challenger II was a professional tennis tournament played on clay courts. It was the fourth edition of the tournament which was part of the 2019 ATP Challenger Tour. It took place in Shymkent, Kazakhstan between 10 and 16 June 2019.

== Tournament details ==

The tournament was played outdoors.
The singles competition field consisted of 48 players.
The doubles competition field consisted of 16 pairs.

¹ Rankings are as of 27 May 2019.
There were 16 seeded players in the singles competition.
The seeded singles players received a bye in the first round.
