# Beipan River Guanxing Highway Bridge

The Beipan River Guanxing Highway Bridge or Beipanjiang River 2003 Bridge is a 366-metre-high suspension bridge on the Guanxing Highway near Xingbei Town (Xinbeizhen), Zhenfeng County, Guizhou Province, China. The bridge has a span length of 388 metres. When it opened in 2003, it was the world's highest bridge, and it held that title until the opening of the Hegigio Gorge Pipeline Bridge in 2005.

When the bridge was completed and opened to traffic in December 2003 it was the highest in the world, sitting 366 metres above the Beipan River.
The bridge became the second Chinese span in two years to take the record.
The first was the Liuguanghe Bridge in 2001.
The bridge was 69 meters higher than the Liuguanhe Bridge.
In 2009 a dam was constructed downstream from the bridge, reducing the drop to the water slightly.
The Beipan River Guanxing Highway Bridge was the first suspension bridge to break the and 300-metre height thresholds (the Liuguanghe Bridge also passes these thresholds but is a beam bridge) as well as the first suspension bridge in the world to surpass the height of Colorado's Royal Gorge Bridge after a 74-year reign.
The bridge is also the second of three Beipan River crossings to have been among the world's ten highest.
The first was the Beipanjiang River Railway Bridge, which opened in 2001 and was the highest train bridge in the world until 2016 when China's Najiehe Railway Bridge and Qinglong Railway Bridge were opened.
The Beipan River Hukun Expressway Bridge, the third bridge to cross high above the Beipanjiang, opened in 2009 on the Guiyang-to-Kunming Highway with a suspension span 1,083 feet (330 m) above the river.
The Beipanjiang Bridge belongs to the fifteen highest bridges in the world.
The Beipanjiang Bridge is still among the fifteen highest bridges in the world.
The bridge is the 11th highest bridge in the world above the obstacle it crosses.
The bridge is the 5th highest bridge in China above the obstacle it crosses.
The Beipanjiang Bridge is the highest bridge in Asia.

== Design and construction ==

The bridge is a two-pylon suspension bridge with a length of 528 meters.
The suspension bridge has two lanes for vehicles and very narrow sidewalks on both sides that are separated from the roadway by stable steel railings.
The road extends to the two steel concrete pylons, which are located immediately at the edge of the gorge and extend approximately 60 meters above the roadway, creating a height difference of about 60 meters between the pylons and the roadway.
The bridge is only slightly longer than its span, and the anchor blocks are located about 100 meters away from the road.
The deck consists of a prestressed concrete slab that is only 60 cm thick, was prefabricated in sections next to the bridge, was lifted into place by a cable crane, and was connected to hangers at its location.
The hangers are attached to the suspension cables and are spaced 5 meters apart.
The edge of the bridge deck was clad with aerodynamically shaped sheets to avoid vibrations.

The bridge tower supports are anchored at the edges of the gorge and are shaped like the letter P.
There are no massive bridge abutments, and only one support descends below the stiffness beam, extending only a short distance beneath the road surface.

== Location and context ==

The bridge crosses the gorge of the Beipanjiang River and is situated at the border of Zhenfeng County and Guangling Buyi and Miao Autonomous County.
The bridge resembles a suspension bridge and is the 117th longest suspension bridge in the world by main span length and the 34th longest suspension bridge in China by main span length.
The suspension bridge is currently located within the area of a reservoir created by the Dongqing Dam, which was completed in 2009 and has a maximum depth of 46 meters.
The stone arch bridge was built in 1961 and had to be replaced because of the reservoir.
The original stone arch bridge was replaced by a concrete arch bridge that carries Provincial Road 210 and spans over the reservoir at sufficient height.
The bridge is physically larger than the suspension bridge.
There is a pedestrian chain bridge located 7 km upstream along the river.
The Guanling–Xinyi expressway is also known as the Guanxing expressway.
