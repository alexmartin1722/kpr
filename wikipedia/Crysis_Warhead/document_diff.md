+Crysis Warhead is a science fiction video game and is the second game released in the Crysis series, though it is not the second part of the Crysis trilogy.[12,13,14] Steven Yeun and Ju Hyun Kim voice Korean soldiers, while Brad Grusnick, Dominic Armato, Kenny Andrews, Nigel Whitmey, Martin Sherman, and Richard Lynson voice Marine soldiers, and Tim Parlett voices a news reporter.
+The AY-69 is clearly visible in the hands of Psycho (the protagonist) on the cover.[22,26,27,28] The game Crysis Warhead contains several Easter eggs, including frogs that form a separate group among them.[43,390] Each of the seven game levels contains one frog that sits on a book.
+In the level 'Call Me Ishmael', a frog is located on one of several small islands near the coastline where a house stands on the coast.
+
+In the level "Coastal Vacation," a frog is located under an overturned boat near a house that is part of an ore processing factory.
+In the level "Frozen Paradise," a frog is located on the roof of one of the buildings.
+In the level "Below Thunder," a frog is located in a ventilation shaft on an underground Korean base.
+One of the frogs is invisible.
+In the level "From the Heart of Hell," a frog is located at the exit of a tunnel through which a train passes with a container and a psychopath on it.
+In the level "All Wrath," a frog is located inside a refrigerator in an air traffic control tower building.
+The protagonist then throws the frog, and the frog explodes after being thrown.
+Collecting all seven frogs changes the final cutscene, and the change to the final cutscene is minor.
+
+A giant-sized frog will sit in place of the alien container.
+The game contains several other Easter eggs, including a level called "Frozen Paradise" where Scrat is frozen into the stone of an ice cave wall together with an acorn, as well as headlines reading "People in German Coburg are bewildered" and "German village chosen as 'Best Party Town in the World'".
