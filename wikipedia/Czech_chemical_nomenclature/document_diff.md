+== Terminology in common language ==
+
+Hydrogen, oxygen, carbon, nitrogen, and silicon are terms that entered common language.
+Examples of such terms include ethanol and etanol.
+
+== Evolution of suffixes ==
+
+Originally, there were five suffixes: -ný, -natý, -itý, -ový, and -elý.
+The expanded set included the suffixes: -ný, -natý, -itý, -ičitý, -ičný, -ečný, -ový, -istý, and -ičelý.
+The suffix used was '-an', which was added to the noun.
+
+== Modernization of terms ==
+
+The old term 'kysličník' was officially replaced by the term 'oxid', the term for carbohydrates changed from 'uhlovodan' to 'sacharid', and the spelling of some elements changed, such as from 'berylium' to 'beryllium'.
