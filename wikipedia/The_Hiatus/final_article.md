According to Hosomi, the HIATUS is a band that wants to create music together with its members.
Hosomi stated that it is interesting that things can be created with the band that cannot be made by one person alone.
Hosomi believes his vocals are just one part of five instrumental parts and considers his vocals not to be the main focus.

Hosomi wants to showcase each member's performance, wrote the lyrics for all the songs, prioritizes creating works meant to be enjoyed for their sound, states that the lyrics contain little message, prioritizes musical aspects over lyrical content, values interesting combinations of consonants and vowels when performed on stage or sung, and states that the most musically suitable lyrics are more important than the text's form or meaning.
The HIATUS is characterized by having different musical styles for each work, features a distinct sound in each work, and has a music system that varies from work to work.
Hosomi was heavily influenced by 1990s alternative rock and grunge, which serve as the core of their sound, while they incorporate post-rock, mass rock, art rock, electronic, techno, and dance music elements into their works.
The HIATUS was formed with the intention of releasing works, and the members gathered specifically to create music.

Initially, the band sought to find the best configuration for all five members to thrive and aimed to explore its identity and determine what kind of band it would be.

== Anomaly ==

From the latter half of the production period of the 2nd album 'ANOMALY', Hosumi could no longer write songs alone, after which all band members began creating songs together and Kashiura co-wrote songs with the main artist for this album, following which the main artist regained the ability to write songs alone.
The album debuted at number one on the Oricon chart, which was the first time the album reached number one on the Oricon chart, and this debut occurred on the first appearance on the chart.
The analog version of the album was delayed until July 14.
The album 'ANOMALY' debuted at number 5 on the Oricon weekly album chart and sold approximately 55,000 copies in its first week.
The catalog number for 'ANOMALY' is FLCF-4340.

== A World of Pandemonium ==

Izawa joined the songwriting process starting with this album, and three people became responsible for composing songs from this point on.
